## Why this page

The keyword map (`docs/keyword-map-2026-11-09.md`) listed small-group tours as the biggest gap: about **1,750 impressions in 3 months** (about 25K over 16 months) for *small group egypt tours*, *egypt group tours* and *group trips to egypt*. It also wrongly said there was no priced product.

There is one. The site sells **four priced, scheduled group tours** (9, 10, 11 and 12 days, from €1,995), and they're all listed on `/small-group-travel-packages`. The page sits at **position 86** because its Google title, "Small Group Travel Packages", doesn't match how people search: they say "tours", plus "Egypt".

## Facts used (from the four products)

- 9 days: Cairo, Abu Simbel overnight, the Nile; groups of up to 12.
- 10 days: Cairo, a Nile cruise from Aswan to Luxor, the Red Sea at Hurghada; up to 12.
- 11 days: Cairo, the Red Sea, a four-night Nile cruise to Aswan; up to 12.
- 12 days: Cairo, Abu Simbel, a dahabiya on the Nile, the Red Sea.
- All four have scheduled departure dates. No group size is claimed for the 12-day tour, so the copy says "a capped group" or "grupo limitado", not a number.
