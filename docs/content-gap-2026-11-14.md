## 3. Priorities

1. **Strengthen the head-term hubs.** These are the existing pages for packages, the Nile cruises hub, the day-tour hubs, small-group and private. For each, set one target term (e.g. "Egypt tour packages", "Nile cruise", "Egypt small group tours", "Egypt private tours", "Luxor day tours", "Cairo day tours"). Make the title and H1 match that term, and add internal links from the relevant guides using that term as the link text. The guide corpus is the authority source.
2. **Push the striking-distance pages to page 1** (table 1, middle row). One page each, mostly title + internal links + FAQ.
3. **Build the missing page types, in order of demand already visible in GSC:**
   a. Duration hubs: 7 / 10 / 12 / 14-day Egypt tours. Our existing itineraries can be grouped under these.
   b. Source-market landing pages: Canada, USA, UK, Australia, South Africa, India. Same tours, local flights/visa/price framing.
   c. Egypt + Jordan and Egypt + Turkey combos. These need a product decision first: do we sell them?
   d. Christmas & New Year and Easter seasonal pages. Publish 6–8 weeks before the season (Christmas is already close).
   e. Honeymoon packages.
   f. Red Sea resort excursion hubs (Hurghada, El Gouna, Makadi, Marsa Alam, Sharm). Egypt Tours Portal's largest advantage.
4. **Low priority / product-dependent:** shore excursions, budget tours, accessible/senior/solo pages.
