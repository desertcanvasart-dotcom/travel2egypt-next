## What went into the city overview (`docs/phase1/copy/t03-siwa`)

These points were missing from the overview and from the sub-pages:

1. **Getting there, one paragraph** (sources: 137052, 112933 "How to get there"). The route via Marsa Matruh, bus or private car, and the option of a night on the coast, with a link to `/guide/siwa-oasis/how-to-go-in-siwa-oasis`. EN and JA put it under "Where Siwa Is". ES already had a "Cómo llegar" note, so the link went there.
2. **Water framing and the salt-lakes links** (137052 "Springs and salt lakes", 112933 "Water, salt lakes, date palms", 205954). Water is the reason Siwa exists, and the salt lakes are tied to drainage, groundwater, water levels and salt damage. Links added to `/guide/siwa-oasis/salt-lakes`, `/guide/siwa-oasis/fatnas-island` and **`/blog/siwa-salt-lakes`** (all three locales).
3. **Etiquette specifics** (205954 "Understand the culture first"). Cover up in town, bring something to wear over swimwear on the way to springs and lakes, ask before photographing anyone, and always before photographing women or children.
4. **Market craft caution** (205954 "Siwa House Museum and crafts", 112933 "Siwa's craft and memory"). Much of the market is made for visitors. See the museum first, ask, then decide. Links to `/guide/siwa-oasis/siwa-house-museum`.
5. **Festivals** (205954 "Festivals and visiting distance", 112933 "Festivals and community", 115042 Dakrour). Siyaha is a reconciliation festival, not a show, and the same applies to weddings and religious occasions. Links to `/guide/siwa-oasis/upcoming-events-in-siwa-oasis`.
6. **When to go** (all four sources). October to April; cold winter nights; summer unsuitable for a first visit, heat-unused travellers, children or older relatives; **Ramadan** (not mentioned anywhere else in the guide). Links to `/guide/siwa-oasis/weather-in-siwa-oasis`. EN and JA get a new section before "How Long to Stay". ES already had a "Temporada" note under "Notas prácticas", so it was extended in place.
7. **Who Siwa suits** (137052 "Who should put Siwa in the itinerary", 205954 "Trips that fit Siwa", 112933). Siwa is not a required stop on a short first trip; see the Nile Valley first. It suits some travellers and not others, stated candidly. New section after "How Long to Stay" in all three locales. Links to `/guide/cairo`, `/guide/giza`, `/guide/luxor`, `/guide/aswan`.

No figures were added. Every existing block is unchanged except where noted below.
