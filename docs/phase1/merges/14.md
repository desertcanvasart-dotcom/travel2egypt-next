## Material worth keeping (not drafted)

**egypt-holiday-deals → hand to the Task 15 drafter (egypt-package-deals).** I didn't create a folder for wp-post-153058-en/-es/-ja, because Task 15 owns that document and two folders for one doc would collide. What package-deals lacks, present in all three locales of holiday-deals:
- The opening framing that Egypt sells two different products (the ancient-Egypt trip and the Red Sea resort trip), and that cheap packages most often fail by mixing them carelessly.
- "Ask for the boat's name." A "five-star cruise" covers a wide range.
- Cheap packages can put souvenir-shop stops ahead of sightseeing, and shared groups can be too large.
- The hidden-costs list: visa, tips, drinks, extra entry and photo tickets, outings such as Abu Simbel, hotel dinners, early breakfast boxes, baggage handling. Also: check what an "all-inclusive" label actually includes. Any figures added later are `[VERIFY]`.
- Red Sea checks: boat-tour quality, house-reef condition, the dive centre, access to protected areas.
- A good guide reads crowding, avoids heat and reorders the visit.

**vacation-in-egypt → personalize-your-egypt-adventure.** Personalize lacks two things:
- The family pacing section: one big visit in the morning, rest in the afternoon; don't put Giza and the GEM on the same day; limit Valley of the Kings tombs; end on the Red Sea.
- The practical-preparation paragraph: visa rules vary, so check official sources; GEM and ticket conditions change; insurance that covers diving and balloon flights; bottled water; dress at religious sites.

If the owner approves the retarget, these can go in as a short section in each locale's draft. The practical items overlap `/travel-tips`, so a link may be better than the text.

**best-places-to-visit-in-egypt and places-in-egypt → `/guide`.** The guide index has no body copy to extend: it renders the Sanity guide-archive settings plus the city list. Material that has no dedicated home on the site today:
- Saqqara and Dahshur as the "experiment before Giza" (both posts).
- Abydos (Seti I temple) and Dendera (Hathor) as trips north from Luxor. `guideArticle.qena.the-temple-of-hathor` exists but is **hidden**.
- Fayoum (Wadi El Hitan, Meidum, Hawara, Lahun, Karanis) and Kharga (Hibis Temple, el-Bagawat).

This is a note for later phases, not something to port now.

**touring-egypt → `/journeys/first-time-in-egypt`.** The first-timer copy is locked, owner-approved text in `src/components/journeys/firstTimeContent.ts` ("DO NOT rewrite, tighten, or expand"), so nothing goes in. Not on that page: the first-trip priority list (Giza, GEM, Karnak, Luxor Temple, Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut; then Medinet Habu, Luxor Museum, Philae, Abu Simbel), and the visa/currency/safety, dress and "should you use a guide" sections. The practical parts are covered by `/travel-tips`.
