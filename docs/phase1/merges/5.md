## What was ported from the source (per locale)

Each locale was ported from its own source post, not translated from EN. ES comes from wp-post-144355-es and JA from wp-post-167055-ja.

- All year: the High Dam regulates the river, and the season changes the feel of the trip rather than the river itself.
- November to February is the easiest window. December and January are crowded and prices climb, and boats fill early. November and February are the balanced months. Book early for small boats and dahabiyas.
- March and April: Easter and school-holiday crowding, spring suits a dahabiya, and late April warms up.
- October as the shoulder, including the Abu Simbel alignment around 22 October (existing source text). Advice for heat-sensitive travellers to wait for November.
- Summer: "day temperatures often go above 40°C" (existing source text, same figure in ES/JA). The advantages, the redesigned day, and boat type in heat (large ship easiest; dahabiya best in spring and autumn; no long felucca trips in summer).
- Crowds and the hour of the day: opening-time visits and which weeks to avoid.
