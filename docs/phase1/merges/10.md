## What was ported from the source (per locale)

ES and JA were ported from their own source posts (wp-post-144356-es, wp-post-167131-ja).

- **Luxor**: the "not just a boarding point, add nights here first" argument. Karnak as a temple city, the Great Hypostyle Hall's 134 columns and Amun. Luxor Temple in the evening. West-bank sites, with new links to Medinet Habu and Deir el-Medina. The "three tombs plus one supplementary" advice.
- **Edfu**: preservation and the full temple sequence, the reliefs (Horus and Seth, ritual, calendar), and the advice to prioritise structure on a short visit.
- **Kom Ombo**: its position by the river, the double dedication to Sobek and Horus the Elder, and the crocodile mummies and museum.
- **Aswan**: Philae on Agilkia, moved stone by stone in the 1970s after the High Dam. The Unfinished Obelisk. The argument for an extra night.
- **Abu Simbel**: not part of a standard cruise, and the ways to get there. The Great Temple and the Nefertari/Hathor temple. The UNESCO-led relocation in the 1960s. The early start and long transfer.
- **What to see on the cruise versus with extra nights**: the whole section, condensed.
