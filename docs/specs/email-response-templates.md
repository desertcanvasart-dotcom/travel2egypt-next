# Travel2Egypt Concierge — Email Response Templates

*For staff handling briefs that arrive from the AI concierge. Adapt these to each visitor; do not send them as-is.*

---

## How to use these templates

Each template is a starting structure for a specific brief shape. The italicized text in brackets is for you to replace with specifics from the actual brief. Do not copy the brackets. Do not send the template without customization.

The templates share a common structure:

1. **Personal opening** — acknowledge the visitor by name and reference one specific thing from their brief
2. **Recap of what we understood** — short, in your own words, demonstrating you actually read the conversation
3. **The proposal or response** — concrete, specific, with pricing
4. **Anticipating concerns** — address one or two things they may be wondering about before they ask
5. **The next step** — clear, with timing and how to reach you
6. **Personal close** — your name, role, direct contact

This structure mirrors the AI's conversational shape so the handoff feels coherent.

Pricing in all templates is in EUR. Adjust deposit terms based on whether the booking is a light service (10%) or a tour package (25% with bespoke later terms).

## A note on tone

These templates are starting points. The voice that lands with each visitor is slightly different. Read the conversation transcript before responding — what was the visitor's energy? Reserved? Effusive? Detail-oriented? Anxious? Match that.

The structure of the templates stays consistent across the rotation. The texture is yours to shape.

---

## When you are uncertain

If a brief lands in your queue and you are not sure which template applies, or the situation is genuinely outside the patterns above:

1. Read the conversation transcript fully first
2. If you can find a reasonable starting point in one of these templates, adapt it
3. If you cannot, write from scratch but stay within the structure (personal opening, recap, proposal, anticipating concerns, next step, personal close)
4. If you are still uncertain, post the transcript in the team Slack/WhatsApp and ask before responding

The visitor's response window is 8 p.m. Cairo same day or 10 a.m. next morning. Within that window, asking for help internally is always better than guessing.
