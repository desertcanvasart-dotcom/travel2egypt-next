Classic Egypt: Cairo, the Nile & Abu Simbel

The whole arc of ancient Egypt in eight days — the Pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum, four nights sailing the Nile from Aswan to Luxor, and the long temples of the south, run privately for your party alone.

Duration8 days · 7 nights
RouteCairo · Aswan · Nile · Luxor
StylePrivate journey
PaceModerate
From€1,290 pp

This is the journey most people mean when they say they want to "see Egypt" — and the eight-day version is the one we send first-time visitors on, because it reaches everything that matters without ever feeling rushed. Two nights in Cairo for the Pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum, a flight south to Aswan, four nights sailing the Nile through the temples of Kom Ombo and Edfu to Luxor, and the great west-bank necropolis at the end. Abu Simbel sits in the middle of it, reached at dawn.

Run privately, the pace bends to you: a slower morning at Karnak, a longer stop at the museum, an earlier start to beat the heat. The cruise is the spine — you unpack once and the country comes to you for four days — and the licensed Egyptologist stays with you the whole way, in Cairo, on the boat, and on the west bank.

The Nile cruise is not a transfer between sights. For four days it is the trip — the temples are simply where you step ashore.

Day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrive Cairo

    Overnight · Cairo

    Met at the airport and transferred to your hotel. The rest of the day is yours to rest after the flight; your guide confirms the plan for the morning.

  2. Day2

    The Pyramids, the Sphinx & the Grand Egyptian Museum

    Overnight · Cairo

    The Giza plateau early, then the Grand Egyptian Museum — now holding the complete Tutankhamun collection in one place for the first time. Old Cairo and Khan el-Khalili if energy allows.

  3. Day3

    Fly to Aswan · board the cruise

    Overnight · Nile cruise

    Morning flight south. The High Dam and the island temple of Philae, then aboard for lunch. The river slows everything down from here.

  4. Day4

    Abu Simbel · sail to Kom Ombo

    Overnight · Nile cruise

    An early excursion to the colossi of Abu Simbel, then back aboard to sail north, with the twin temple of Kom Ombo at dusk.

  5. Day5

    Edfu · sail to Luxor

    Overnight · Nile cruise

    The best-preserved temple in Egypt at Edfu in the morning, then a long, slow sail to Luxor through the country the cruise exists for.

  6. Day6

    The Valley of the Kings & the west bank

    Overnight · Luxor

    The royal tombs, Hatshepsut's temple and the Colossi of Memnon. Disembark and into a Luxor hotel for the last two nights ashore.

  7. Day7

    Karnak & Luxor Temple · fly to Cairo

    Overnight · Cairo

    The vast east-bank temples in the cooler morning, then an afternoon flight back to Cairo for the final night.

  8. Day8

    Departure

    Transfer out

    A private transfer to the airport in time for your flight home.

Concierge note

The eight-day version is the one to choose if it's your first time. If you've seen Cairo before, we drop the first two days and start you on the river — say so and we'll re-cost it.

How we pace it, and when to go

October to April is the comfortable window; we start mornings early year-round to keep the temples ahead of both the heat and the coaches. The cruise can run Aswan-to-Luxor or the reverse depending on sailing dates — the itinerary above is the more common direction, and we confirm yours when we hold the boat.

Who it's for — and who should think twice

This is the right first journey for almost anyone: it's comprehensive, comfortably paced, and the cruise removes most of the daily packing and transfers. Think twice only if you have less than a week — in which case a shorter Nile-focused trip serves better — or if you want to go deep on one region rather than see the whole arc. Tell us either way and we'll point you to the journey that fits.
