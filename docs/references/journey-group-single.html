This is Egypt's greatest hits, done properly and in good company — the Pyramids and the Egyptian Museum in Cairo, the temples of Luxor, a stretch of the Nile by boat, and the gentle south at Aswan, in nine days that move briskly without ever feeling rushed. It's our scheduled small-group departure: a fixed itinerary, a guaranteed Egyptologist, and no more than twelve travellers, so you see the country with a handful of like-minded people rather than alone or in a coach of fifty.

We built it for travellers who have a week or so and want the famous places without compromise — the sites everyone comes for, with the logistics, internal flights and hotels arranged, and a set price you can book today against a published date.

The shape of the days

Two nights in Cairo to start, a flight south to the river, two nights sailing between the great temples, and the last nights in Aswan and back via Luxor. Mornings are early to stay ahead of the heat and the crowds; afternoons soften.

Arrive Cairo

Overnight · Cairo

Met at the airport and transferred to the hotel. An evening to settle; the group dinner is optional.

The Pyramids & the Egyptian Museum

Overnight · Cairo

Giza and the Sphinx in the morning, the Museum's highlights after lunch — the headline day, taken at a human pace.

Fly south · board the Nile

Overnight · cruise

Morning flight to Luxor and aboard the ship; the east-bank temples in the afternoon.

Luxor's west bank

Overnight · cruise

The Valley of the Kings and Hatshepsut's terraces, then sail upstream.

Edfu & Kom Ombo

Overnight · cruise

Two temples on the riverbank, a long afternoon on deck between them.

Aswan

Overnight · Aswan

Philae temple on its island and a felucca round Elephantine as the light goes.

Aswan at leisure (Abu Simbel optional)

Overnight · Aswan

A free morning, or the optional flight south to Abu Simbel for those who want it.

Return to Luxor & Cairo

Overnight · Cairo

Back north by air, with any loose ends of Luxor on the way.
