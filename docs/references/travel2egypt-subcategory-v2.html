Editor's view

Sharm is not one destination. It is three different doors.

Use this page to decide whether your free day belongs to the reef, the mountain, or the Bedouin interior — then pick the tour that matches your energy.

Curated by the Travel2Egypt Sinai team, for travellers who want shared costs without losing the feeling of a guided, personal day.

Sharm El-Sheikh is often sold as a beach resort, but the better way to understand it is as a threshold. One side opens to the coral gardens of Ras Mohammed and the Blue Hole; another leads inland to the granite of St. Catherine; a third slips into wadis, mangroves and Bedouin camps that feel far removed from hotel life.

Our small group days are built for travellers who want the practicality of a fixed departure without the anonymity of a large coach. Ten guests is small enough to ask questions, stop properly, and feel the guide is shaping the day around people rather than managing numbers.

If Cairo is where Egypt overwhelms you with history, Sharm is where Egypt opens sideways — into reef, mountain, silence and night sky.

Who this works for

Couples, solo travellers and families who already have a Red Sea hotel booked and want one or two memorable days beyond the beach — especially if you like nature, light adventure, photography, snorkelling, or the idea of seeing St. Catherine and Mount Sinai without arranging a fully private journey.

Who should think twice

If your main interest is pharaonic archaeology, Sharm is not the natural base — Cairo, Luxor and Aswan will serve you better. From Sharm, the strongest experiences are natural and spiritual rather than museum-like: reef walls, desert tracks, monastery mornings, and the physical effort of the Mount Sinai climb.

How the days spread out

Easy on the coast, slower in the mountains.

The reef days feel natural from a beach hotel — an hour out, back for dinner. The mountain days are deeper and more demanding, starting late evening or before dawn. That difference is the thing to plan around.

  • Easy dayRas Mohammed, or a relaxed snorkelling boat
  • Active dayDahab, Wadi Qunai and the Blue Hole
  • Signature dayMount Sinai sunrise & St. Catherine's Monastery
