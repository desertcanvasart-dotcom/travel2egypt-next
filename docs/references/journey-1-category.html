Private Day Tours in Egypt

Guided days run for your party alone — no group to wait on, no fixed script, a licensed Egyptologist, and the pace set by you. Nine cities, every kind of day.

Why private

A private day is the difference between visiting Egypt and being moved through it.

Read this to understand how we run a day, then choose the city you'll be in. Each one opens to its own days.

A private day runs for your party alone — two people or twelve — with a dedicated guide and vehicle, no minimum group size, and no waiting on other bookings. You decide where the long part of the afternoon goes; the guide reads the place; you set the pace. That is the whole proposition, and it is a different thing entirely from a seat on a coach.

The point of a private day is not luxury. It is that the day bends around you, instead of you bending around the day.

Who it suits

Anyone who would rather not share a coach; anyone travelling with children or on limited time; anyone who wants to ask real questions and get real answers. It costs more than a group seat. It is worth it for most travellers and essential for some — and we will tell you honestly when a small-group day would serve you just as well for less.

How a private day works

The day comes in tiers, so you only pay for what you want. Entrance fees in Egypt are revised often — keeping them separate keeps the base fare honest.

  • BasePrivate vehicle, licensed Egyptologist guide, water, all logistics. The day itself.
  • Base + EntranceThe above, plus entry to every ticketed site on the route.
  • Base + Entrance + LunchThe full day — sites included, plus a proper sit-down lunch at a vetted spot.

In season now

October to March is the comfortable window for full days outdoors — the Valley of the Kings, Saqqara and the desert are kindest before noon in any season.
