Egypt Travel Packages

The whole country, properly — days that join into a journey, with the hotels, the cruise, the flights and the guide all arranged before you arrive. Ten ways to spend a week or more.

Why a journey

A package is not a longer tour. It is the difference between seeing the sights and seeing the country.

Read this to understand how we build a journey, then choose the kind of trip you're after — each opens to its own set.

A day tour shows you one place; a journey shows you Egypt — the way the Pyramids give onto the Nile, the way the river carries you from Cairo to the temples of the south, the way a week settles into a rhythm of moving and resting. We build these as private journeys for your party alone, or as scheduled small-group departures, with every hotel, transfer, internal flight and the licensed Egyptologist arranged in advance.

On a good journey you unpack as little as possible and let the country come to you — most of all on the river, where the cruise is the trip, not the transfer.

Who it suits

First-time visitors who want the whole arc without assembling it themselves; returning travellers going deeper into one region; families and couples who'd rather have the logistics handled than improvise across a country this size. If you only have a free day or two from a beach, you want a day tour, not a journey — we'll say so.

How the journeys are organised

By the shape of the trip, not the map. The cruises sit together; the in-depth circuits together; the family, luxury, adventure and special-interest journeys each in their own set — so you start from the kind of week you want, then narrow to the itinerary.

  • PrivateYour party alone, your own dates, the pace set by you.
  • Small groupScheduled departures shared with a handful of others — fixed dates, lower cost.
