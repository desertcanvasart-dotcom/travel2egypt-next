## About this experience

Siwa has its own rhythm. This journey gives you the time to feel it — floating across salt lakes, wandering through places shaped by centuries, and watching the light disappear over the Great Sand Sea.

Mornings are slow. Each day holds one or two moments, never more, and ends where the light is best. From your Travel2Egypt stay to every drive across the oasis, each detail is arranged so you can simply be present.

## Included

- 3 nights at your Travel2Egypt stay in Siwa

- Salt & Spring Escape — Cleopatra Spring and a salt lake float at sunset

## Day 1 — Salt & Springs

Arrive to a slow Siwa morning, with breakfast and time to settle in at your Travel2Egypt stay. In the afternoon, Cleopatra Spring, then a quiet salt lake as the sun goes down.

- **7:00 AM · Arrival** — Morning arrival at your Travel2Egypt stay after the overnight drive. Rest, a shower, the courtyard.
- **8:30 AM · Breakfast** — Dates, olives, fresh bread and mint tea. The rest of the morning is yours.
- **2:30 PM · Cleopatra Spring** — Cool, clear water in a stone-carved pool shaded by palms.
- **3:45 PM · Salt lake** — A lake chosen because it stays quiet at this hour. Towels and water laid out. You float without trying.
- **4:45 PM · Sunset on the salt** — The water turns the colour of the sky.
- **Evening · Without a schedule** — Dinner at leisure. Nothing planned, on purpose.

## Day 2 — Stories of Old Siwa

Step into Siwa's past with a private guide. Tombs and the Oracle in the cool of the morning, the town and its stories through the afternoon, and Shali at dusk.

- **9:30 AM · Mountain of the Dead** — Painted rock-cut tombs, and the whole oasis spread out from the top.
- **10:45 AM · Oracle Temple** — Where Alexander came to consult Amun in 332 BC. Then the Temple of Amun at Umm Ubeida nearby.
- **12:30 PM · Lunch** — A local spot your guide trusts.
- **2:00 PM · Siwa House Museum** — Embroidery, silver and everyday life in a traditional karsheef house.
- **2:45 PM · Black Lions Library** — A small private library kept by an Italian traveller who has lived in Siwa for more than twenty years. Rare books on the oasis, his photographs of Siwa across two decades, and a small room screening documentaries of how the oasis used to look.
- **3:30 PM · Shali & the Old Town** — The old salt-and-mud fortress, the lanes and the market.
- **5:00 PM · Sunset over Shali** — From the rooftop, as the fortress turns gold.

## Day 3 — Into the Great Sand Sea

A free morning. Then leave the oasis behind by private 4×4 and head deep into the Great Sand Sea — a hot spring in the dunes, sandboarding, sunset, then dinner and stars.

- **1:30 PM · Departure** — Private 4×4 from your Travel2Egypt stay. Permits arranged, water and fruit on board.
- **2:45 PM · Fossil beds** — Shells from the ancient sea that once covered the desert.
- **3:30 PM · Bir Wahed** — A warm spring pool surrounded by dunes.
- **4:15 PM · Sandboarding** — The best slope of the day. No experience needed.
- **4:45 PM · Sunset tea** — Carpets, a small fire and tea on the highest dune as the light gives out.
- **6:30 PM · Dinner under the stars** — Fire-cooked, at a private camp.
- **7:45 PM · Stargazing** — Constellations, the myths behind them, and a telescope under one of the darkest skies in North Africa.
- **9:30 PM · Return** — Back to your stay beneath the stars, or stay the night in the desert (optional).

## Day 4 — A Slow Goodbye

A final breakfast at your Travel2Egypt stay and an unhurried morning before the journey back to Cairo.

- **8:00 AM · Breakfast** — No alarm, no rush.
- **Late morning · Last walk** — Dates and olive oil to take home, or simply the quiet.
- **11:00 AM · Departure** — Private transfer to Cairo, with a lunch stop by the sea at Marsa Matrouh.

## Practical information

Times shown are for November–February. From March to April and in October, afternoon moments move later so sunset stays in its place.

### What to bring

- Swimwear — for the spring, the salt lake and the hot spring
- Comfortable walking shoes, and closed shoes for the dunes
- Light layers, and a warm one for desert evenings
- Sunglasses and sun protection
- Modest clothing for the town — shoulders and knees covered
- Egyptian Pounds for the market

### Things guests ask

**How do we get to Siwa?** Private transportation from your preferred location in Cairo is arranged as part of the journey, with an overnight departure for a morning arrival in Siwa.

**Can the journey be personalised?** Yes. The pace, stay and selected experiences can be tailored for private journeys.

**Where will we stay?** All three nights are hosted at a Travel2Egypt stay in Siwa, selected according to availability and the character of your journey.

**Do I need to know how to swim?** No. The salt lake holds you, and Cleopatra Spring is shallow enough to stand in.

**Can we sleep in the desert?** Yes. On Day 3 you can stay at a private camp instead of returning to your stay — bedding by the fire, sunrise over the dunes, breakfast before the drive back. Ask when you request the journey.

**What will we eat?** Breakfast daily at your Travel2Egypt stay, and a fire-cooked dinner in the desert on Day 3. Other meals are at leisure — your guide knows where to go. Tell us about any dietary needs and we'll plan around them.

### Meeting point

Private pickup from your preferred location in Cairo. Exact departure time and driver details are shared before the journey.

### Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. 50% refund 7–14 days before. Non-refundable within 7 days. Full credit toward a future Travel2Egypt journey on request.
