Key decisions:
- Unified day model: morning (PT) + lunch (i18n string) + afternoon (PT) as the per-day shape regardless of dayTour (1-entry days[]) vs package (N-entry days[]).
- Three mapper extraction paths driven by structural detection: modern uses nested-tabs[0..3] for Overview/Itinerary/Dates/FAQs; older walks flat sections for Overview heading + accordion; private-car-and-guide routes the single-tab accordion content to top-level body (not days[]) and extracts pricing from a content-matched html widget.
- 9 classifier reroutes to EXPLICIT_PAGE_ROUTING → tour-or-package: 4 from Phase 2b.d.1.fix (slugs reversed out of EXPLICIT_DEFER_SLUGS — session 6.5a 8r-2b/8r-2c decision reversal) + 5 from Phase 2b.d.1.fix.2 (Session 6 D5 slugs whose -egypt suffix was being caught by destination-token rule before TOUR_PATTERNS could route them).
- SLUG_CITY_OVERRIDES (1 entry): snorkeling-adventure-on-the-nefertari-submarine → marsa-alam. Pattern reusable for future water-based/named-attraction tours where slug-prefix matching fails.
- Schema visibility: days[] hidden when dayTourMode === 'private'. Highlights / Inclusions / Exclusions / Legacy itinerary remain visible regardless.
- Content extraction rules locked in Phase 2b.d.4 from Studio QA:
  • summary built from first body PT block (≥50 chars) with word-boundary truncation at 240, replacing the WP-excerpt-based path that leaked Elementor tab labels.
  • Modern package priceIndication empty by design (Dates & Prices tab is transactional pricing-schedule data, not editorial).
  • Private-car priceIndication clipped at "Group Size" marker (drops pax-break pricing matrix, keeps package descriptions).
  • Day titles get leading-punctuation strip (:;-–— + whitespace) to handle accordion source markup where the colon sits outside the bold span.
- Gallery extraction logic preserved in extractOlder but emission disabled at doc-construction (sourceUrl stubs aren't valid Sanity image refs; real asset upload deferred to a later sub-phase).

Lessons:

- internationalizedArrayString cannot nest inside `array of internationalizedArrayString`. Mirror the top-level highlights pattern instead.
- Architect-reviewer pattern with STOP gates handled 9 sub-iterations cleanly across 4 phase boundaries.
- Studio QA round 1 caught 4 content-quality issues invisible to dry-run + harness validation. Dry-run gates correctness; manual Studio QA gates content quality. Both layers stay in the loop for first cohort of each new content type.
