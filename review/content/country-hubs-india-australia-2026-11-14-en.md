## Hub 1: `/egypt-tours-from-india`
**SEO title:** Egypt Tours from India: Private Journeys of 7 to 14 Days
**Meta description:** Egypt tours for travellers from India: seven to fourteen days across Cairo, Luxor, Aswan and the Nile, private and starting on any date.
**H1:** Egypt Tours from India

**Lede:** From India, most travellers reach Cairo through Doha, Dubai or Kuala Lumpur, so the trip starts with a long day of travel. The tours here run seven to fourteen days, private and starting on any date.

**Heading:** Planning the trip from India
> Most flights from India to Egypt connect through Doha, Dubai or Kuala Lumpur. Check the connection time and the arrival hour in Cairo, not just the airline: after a late-night arrival, treat the first day as rest.
>
> Seven days is the shortest trip that connects Cairo with Luxor and Aswan properly. Ten days adds Abu Simbel and time to go deeper at each place.
>
> October to April is the comfortable season for sightseeing. December and January are the busiest and cost more.

**FAQ:**
1. **Do travellers from India need a visa for Egypt?** Travellers from India can get a visa on arrival in Egypt. Eligibility rules and fees change, so check Egypt's official e-visa site before you go.
2. **Should we rest in Cairo after arriving?** Usually, yes. After a long journey with a connection, a night in Cairo before heading south is easier than travelling on the same day.
3. **Do prices change by season?** Yes. Christmas, New Year and Easter are peak dates in Egypt. Scheduled departures on those dates usually cost 30% more, and private journeys cost more over the same dates too.
