## Owner facts (given 2026-11-14)

1. **Peak pricing.** Peak dates are **Christmas, New Year and Easter**. Flagged departures are **usually 30%** higher. Private trips also cost more on these dates (no single percentage given, so none is stated).
2. **Booking lead time.** **60 days** for Christmas and New Year.
3. **Gala dinners.** Most Egyptian hotels hold a gala dinner and it's **compulsory, no exception** (read as: charged on top of the room rate). Nile cruises also hold one, but it's **included in the cruise rate**.
4. **Christmas.** The gala is on **24 December** (Christmas Eve), not the 25th. Site opening on 25 December wasn't confirmed, so the page doesn't mention it.

## Draft EN copy

**SEO title:** Christmas and New Year in Egypt: Holiday Tours and Departures
**Meta description:** Spend Christmas and New Year in Egypt: scheduled small-group departures from 23 December, or a private journey on your own dates. Book early. It's the busiest fortnight of the year.
**H1:** Christmas and New Year in Egypt

**Lede:**
> The fortnight from Christmas to New Year is the busiest of Egypt's year. That isn't a reason to avoid it; it's a reason to book early. The weather is at its easiest, Nile cruises are in their main season, and a ten-day break is enough for Cairo, the river and Aswan.

**Why section heading:** Why Egypt at Christmas
> Winter is the season that makes Egypt easiest to see: the heat drops, and the Giza plateau, Karnak and the Valley of the Kings can be read rather than endured.
>
> The trade-off is crowds. December into January is peak season. Hotels, boats and guides fill, and the popular dahabiyas go first.
>
> Egypt's own Christmas comes later. 7 January is Christmas for the Coptic Orthodox Church. If you're in Cairo then, Coptic Cairo gains depth, but it's a time of worship, not a tourist event.

**Picks heading:** Holiday departures we'd book ourselves.

**FAQ:**
1. **When should we book a Christmas trip to Egypt?** At least 60 days ahead for Christmas and New Year. Hotels, boats and guides fill first over this fortnight. Closer than that, ask us anyway: availability is limited, but not always gone.
2. **Are prices higher over Christmas and New Year?** Yes. Christmas, New Year and Easter are peak dates in Egypt. Scheduled departures on those dates usually cost 30% more. Private journeys cost more over the same dates too.

   ⚠️ *Correction pending owner OK (2026-11-14):* removed "and the departure prices shown here already include it". It isn't true: the holiday group tours show base "From" prices ($2,200–$3,540) and their departure rows say "on request". Removal only; nothing added.
3. **Is there a gala dinner on Christmas Eve or New Year's Eve?** Most Egyptian hotels hold a gala dinner on Christmas Eve (24 December) and on New Year's Eve, and it is compulsory, charged on top of the room rate with no exception. On a Nile cruise the gala dinner is included in the cruise price. That difference is worth knowing when you compare a hotel stay with a cruise over the holidays.
4. **Is December a good time for a Nile cruise?** Yes. Winter is the main Nile cruise season. Ships and quays are busier, so confirm the boat, cabin and sightseeing times in advance. For a quieter river, dahabiyas sail in winter too, but the popular boats fill early.
5. **What is Coptic Christmas?** 7 January is Christmas for Egypt's Coptic Orthodox Church, with services at churches across the country, including St. Mark's Cathedral in Abbasiya. It's a religious occasion, not a tourist event; dress and behave as you would at any place of worship.
6. **Can we do a shorter trip over the holidays?** Yes. A private journey can start on any date. Tell us the days you have and we'll shape it around them.
