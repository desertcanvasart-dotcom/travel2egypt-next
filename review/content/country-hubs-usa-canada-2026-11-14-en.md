## Hub 1: `/egypt-tours-from-usa`
**SEO title:** Egypt Tours from the USA: Private Journeys and Small-Group Departures
**Meta description:** Egypt tours for travellers from the USA: seven to fourteen days across Cairo, Luxor, Aswan and the Nile, private on any date or with a small group from the USA and Canada.
**H1:** Egypt Tours from the USA

**Lede:** From the USA, the long flight shapes the trip: under a week on the ground in Egypt becomes rushed. The tours here run seven to fourteen days, private on any date, or as a small-group departure planned for travellers from the USA and Canada.

**Heading:** Planning the trip from the USA
> From around New York, a direct flight to Cairo can be an option. From most other US cities, travellers connect through a European, Middle Eastern or Turkish hub, so check the connection time and the arrival hour, not just the airline.
>
> Plan at least seven nights for Cairo and Luxor. Ten to twelve days lets Cairo, Luxor, Aswan, a Nile cruise and Abu Simbel fit together, and twelve to fourteen leaves room for a few days on the Red Sea.
>
> October to April is the comfortable season. November, February and March balance weather and crowds; December and January are busier and cost more.

**FAQ:**
1. **Do US citizens need a visa for Egypt?** Yes. US citizens need a visa to enter Egypt, and tourists can apply for an e-visa or get one on arrival. Conditions and prices can change, so check Egypt's official e-visa site and US State Department travel information before you go.
2. **Should we stop in Cairo before heading south?** Usually, yes. After a long-haul flight, a night in Cairo before Luxor or Aswan is easier than connecting the same day, when delays and fatigue add up.
3. **Do prices change by season?** Yes. Christmas, New Year and Easter are peak dates in Egypt. Scheduled departures on those dates usually cost 30% more, and private journeys cost more over the same dates too.

**H1:** Egypt Tours from Canada

**Heading:** Planning the trip from Canada
> EgyptAir flies direct from Toronto Pearson to Cairo. From Montreal, Vancouver, Calgary or Ottawa, travellers compare connections through Toronto, Europe or the Middle East, and the transfer time and arrival hour matter more than the airline.
>
> A workable first trip: two or three nights in Cairo, two or three in Luxor, three or four for a Nile cruise or Aswan, and two or three on the Red Sea at the end.
>
> October to April is the easiest window. February is a particularly good choice from Canada: you leave the cold behind and find walkable temperatures for sightseeing.

**FAQ:**
1. **Do Canadians need a visa for Egypt?** Yes. Canadian passport holders need a tourist visa. You can apply for an e-visa in advance or get a 30-day tourist visa on arrival, usually paid in US-dollar cash. Rules change, so check Egypt's official e-visa site and Canadian government travel advice before you go.
2. **Is there a direct flight from Canada?** EgyptAir flies direct from Toronto Pearson to Cairo. From other Canadian cities, you'll connect through Toronto, Europe or the Middle East.
3. **Do prices change by season?** Yes. Christmas, New Year and Easter are peak dates in Egypt. Scheduled departures on those dates usually cost 30% more, and private journeys cost more over the same dates too.
