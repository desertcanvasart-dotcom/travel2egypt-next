# Journey pages, Phase A: owner review (EN)

**How to review:** tick ☐ → ☑ to approve a row. To correct a row, write the corrected wording after it (e.g. `→ 8–10 days`). Rows you leave unticked and uncorrected are rejected and deleted. Nothing here renders on the site until you approve it.

**How the tours were ranked:**
- **Rule:** published, priced, private multi-day packages from the paired theme hub. They are ranked by **GA4 pageviews, last 90 days** (property Travel2Egypt, "Pages and screens", 12 Aug – 9 Nov 2026, EN path), then by price spread.
- **Caveat:** GA4 volumes are very low, because analytics only runs after cookie consent, so a difference of one or two views decides little. Treat the ranking as a weak tie-breaker.
- **Quotes:** each "why it suits" line is written from the product's own text, and the source sentence is quoted exactly, from the product's audience note or summary in Sanity.
- **Prices:** shown in EUR as stored (the site converts them).

**Images:**
- Each hero is an existing Sanity image from one of the proposed tours.
- Sanity stores **no credit or source** for these assets. Their filenames match the product slugs from the company's WordPress library, so they look like your own photos, but **please confirm ownership before approving**.
- Thumbnails open the Sanity CDN.
