## Opening

Not every Egypt trip is built around temples. This one is built around water: five nights on the Red Sea at Hurghada, with the Giftun reefs offshore and the wrecks further out, then two nights in Cairo for the Citadel, Khan el-Khalili, the pyramids of Giza and the Grand Egyptian Museum, with your own Egyptologist.

---

## Why this route works

The coast comes first, while you have the energy for early boats and long days in the water, and the days there are deliberately open. Cairo comes last and carries the structure: a short flight west, two guided days ending at the pyramids, and the city’s noise and density as a deliberate contrast to the week before. Hurghada loosens the days; Cairo, at the end, puts the country back in focus.

### Day 2 · Hurghada
**The first day in the water**

The reef starts close to the shore here, and the first full day is for finding out what that means. Snorkel over the coral gardens near the beach, take a guided introductory dive or, if you’re certified, a dive off a boat; windsurf across the bay; or simply use the beach, which needs no justification. The resort’s facilities are yours throughout, and there is no obligation to leave them.

### Day 3 · Hurghada
**A free day, or the Giftun Islands**

For many visitors, the reason to come. Big and Small Giftun lie a short crossing offshore, inside a marine protected area, with reef walls and shallow coral gardens for snorkelling and the usual lunch mooring at Mahmya Bay on the south side of Big Giftun. A full day out on a boat, back at the resort by late afternoon.

**Overnight:** Hurghada resort, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** As much or as little as you like

### Day 4 · Hurghada
**The wrecks, or a slower day**

Wreck diving is Hurghada’s particular offering. For certified divers, the SS Thistlegorm in the Strait of Gubal, north of the port, sunk in October 1941 with its cargo of trucks and motorcycles, now overgrown with coral: a long day and a deeper dive. Other wrecks lie closer to shore as half-day trips. For non-divers, a glass-bottom boat shows the reef from above, and the day can equally go to the spa.

**The day:** Your choice
**Overnight:** Hurghada resort, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** As much or as little as you like

**Optional on this day:**
- A wreck dive, the Thistlegorm or closer to shore, for certified divers: extra
- A glass-bottom boat: extra

### Day 5 · Hurghada
**El Dahar, and a final open day**

The last coastal day is left open by design. By now you know whether you want one more reef morning, a dolphin-watching boat or a quad bike into the desert behind the coast. A morning in El Dahar, the old town behind the resort strip, is worth the time: the covered market, the fishing quarter that predates the resorts, and the cafés along the waterfront, the parts of Hurghada that didn’t grow out of tourism.

**The day:** Your choice

**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner

### Day 6 · Hurghada → Cairo
**The Citadel, Khan el-Khalili and a felucca on the Nile**

A short domestic flight west to Cairo, and the trip changes register entirely. Your Egyptologist meets you for the Citadel of Saladin, the medieval fortress on Cairo’s eastern ridge since the 12th century, and the alabaster Mosque of Muhammad Ali inside its walls, completed in the 1840s. Then down into Khan el-Khalili, the bazaar trading since the late 14th century, and in the evening a felucca on the Nile, slow and quiet, with the city on both banks.

**The day:** Citadel of Saladin · Mosque of Muhammad Ali · Khan el-Khalili · felucca on the Nile
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast (at the resort) · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfer to Hurghada airport · domestic flight Hurghada → Cairo (not included) · private transfers · felucca

### Day 7 · Giza
**The pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum**

Giza in the morning: the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC. You go inside the pyramid of Khafre or Menkaure, subject to site rules and availability on the day, then the panorama viewpoint, where the three pyramids line up in one frame, and the Great Sphinx with the Valley Temple below it. After lunch, the Grand Egyptian Museum, beside the Giza plateau, where the complete Tutankhamun collection is shown together. It deserves the whole afternoon.

**The day:** Giza pyramids · inside Khafre or Menkaure · panorama viewpoint · Great Sphinx · Valley Temple · Grand Egyptian Museum
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers

**Optional on this day:**
- Inside the Great Pyramid of Khufu: extra ticket, subject to availability and site rules on the day
- A camel ride at the panorama area: extra cost. We work with handlers we know, whose animals are in good condition, and the ride is entirely your choice.
