- **Tickets** follow the same Saqqara rule as the other Memphis tours.

## Figures

- Memphis around 3100 BC
- Djoser around 2650 BC; enclosure wall over 1 km
- Bent Pyramid around 2600 BC, 54° to 43°
- Red Pyramid 104 m (see the small point above)
- About 30 km between the three sites; about 8 hours
