## Opening

There are no temples on this trip and no Nile. It reads Egypt in geological time instead: the fossil whales of Wadi al-Hitan, left by a sea that covered the Fayoum some forty million years ago, and further west the chalk of the White Desert, carved by the wind. Four days, two nights under the desert sky and one in an oasis between them.

---

## Why this route works

It moves southwest from Cairo in two stages. The Fayoum comes first, close to the city, with its museum, its lakes and the whale valley in one long day. Then the crossing to Bahariya, an afternoon in the oasis and a hotel night, before a full day by 4×4 through the Black Desert to the White Desert and a camp among the formations. The last day is the road home. Two guides travel with you: an Egyptologist for the history and a desert guide for the desert.

## Your four days

### Day 1 · Cairo → the Fayoum
**Karanis, the Wadi al-Rayan falls and the Valley of the Whales**

Southwest from Cairo to the Fayoum, a green basin fed by the Bahr Yussef canal. The museum at Kom Aushim stands on Karanis, a Graeco-Roman town founded in the 3rd century BC. Then Wadi al-Rayan, a protected area where small waterfalls, fed by drainage from the farmland, run between two desert lakes. In the afternoon, Wadi al-Hitan, inscribed by UNESCO in 2005: skeletons of early whales some forty million years old, still with small hind legs, left where they lie in the rock, with walking paths and a visitor centre. The night is at a desert camp.

**The day:** Kom Aushim museum · Karanis · Wadi al-Rayan · Wadi al-Hitan
**Overnight:** Desert camp in the Fayoum
**Meals:** Lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private car, Cairo → Fayoum · private 4×4 on the desert track to Wadi al-Hitan
**Pace:** A long first day

---

### Day 2 · The Fayoum → Bahariya
**Across the desert to the oasis**

About four hours across the open desert to Bahariya, green with palms and olives around the town of Bawiti, in its depression some 370 kilometres southwest of Cairo. In the afternoon, Pyramid Mountain, named for its shape and not for any pharaoh, with a wide view over the oasis; English Mountain, with the ruins of a British post from the First World War; and the Ethnic Museum, with objects from oasis life. A hot spring at dusk, then your hotel.

**The day:** Pyramid Mountain · English Mountain · Ethnic Museum · hot spring
**Overnight:** Bahariya hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private 4×4, Fayoum → Bahariya
**Pace:** A long drive, then an easy afternoon

---

### Day 3 · Bahariya → the White Desert
**The Black Desert, Crystal Mountain, Agabat and a night among the chalk**

By 4×4 south through the Black Desert, its hills capped with dark basalt, to lunch at El Haiz. Crystal Mountain, an outcrop of quartz crystals exposed by erosion, and the Agabat valley, its pale spires best walked rather than seen from the road. Then the White Desert National Park, where the wind has carved the chalk into Mushroom Rock, towers and columns, with long shadows at dusk. Camp among them, with dinner over the fire.

**The day:** Black Desert · El Haiz · Crystal Mountain · Agabat · White Desert National Park
**Overnight:** Camping in the White Desert
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private 4×4

---

### Day 4 · The White Desert → Cairo
**Breakfast in the desert, and the road home**

Breakfast at camp as the light comes up over the chalk. Then north by 4×4 to Bahariya and on by car to Cairo, a long drive that brings you back to your hotel in the afternoon.

**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Private 4×4 and car, White Desert → Bahariya → Cairo
**Pace:** A long drive

## Is this journey for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- are drawn to geology, fossils and silence more than to monuments
- have seen the Nile and want the other Egypt
- have only a short window for the desert

**Worth knowing:**
- There are no pharaonic temples on this trip.
- Two of the three nights are camping, and the camps are simple: tents, a fire, dinner and the stars.
- Days 1, 2 and 4 involve long drives.
- Desert nights can be cold, especially in winter. Bring warm layers.
