## Opening

Most travellers see Egypt as a thin green ribbon along the Nile. This private two-day trip leaves it behind: west from Cairo to the Bahariya Oasis, then by 4×4 through the Black Desert, past Crystal Mountain and the cliffs of Agabat, into the chalk formations of the White Desert, and a night in a desert camp.

---

## Why this route works

The early start is what makes two days enough. Leaving Cairo at 05:00 puts you in Bahariya by mid-morning, so the whole of Day 1 goes to the desert itself, with the White Desert reached in the late afternoon. The night is spent at a camp, and Day 2 is the drive home. The trip skips Bahariya’s antiquities to give the time to the landscape, and it’s led by a Bedouin guide who knows the desert.

## Your two days

### Day 1 · Cairo → the Black and White Deserts

**05:00 · Pickup from your hotel in Cairo**
Your guide and driver meet you in the hotel lobby with a sign bearing your name.

**The drive to Bahariya**
About 370 kilometres south-west of Cairo, roughly four hours on the road. In Bahariya you change to a 4×4 for the desert.

**Lunch in Bahariya**

**The Black Desert: Al-Marsous Mountain**
One of the best-known mountains of the Black Desert, a volcanic crater covered in dark stone.

**The Bedouin village and Crystal Mountain**
A stop at a Bedouin village to see its cold sulphur-water well, then Crystal Mountain in the Agabat area, a ridge studded with crystal.

**The White Desert National Park**
The reason most people make this trip. Wind and sand have carved the white chalk into giant mushrooms, a chicken, a rabbit and countless smaller shapes, scattered across the plain.

**Evening · The camp at El Haiz**
Dinner and the night in a tent at the campsite at El Haiz. If you’d rather not sleep in a tent, you can spend the night in a hotel instead, at the same price.

## Is this journey for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- want a night in the desert as a break from Cairo and the Nile
- are curious about Egypt beyond the river
- take photographs

**Worth knowing:**
- Pickup is at 05:00, and the first day is long: about four hours’ drive before the desert even starts.
- The camp is simple: you sleep in a tent, and there are no toilets. A toilet tent can be added on request, or you can take the hotel option instead.
- Your guide is a Bedouin who speaks English. If you need a guide in another language, or an Egyptologist, ask when you book.
- Desert nights can be cold, especially in winter. Bring warm layers.
- This trip doesn’t visit Bahariya’s antiquities, such as the Golden Mummies.
