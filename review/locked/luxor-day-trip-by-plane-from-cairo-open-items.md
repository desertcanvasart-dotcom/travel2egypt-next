# Luxor in a Day by Plane from Cairo: open items

Source: the live page `/full-day-trip-to-luxor-by-plane-from-cairo`, read 17 Nov 2026. Owner answers received 22 Nov 2026.

## Resolved (22 Nov), applied in all three languages

- **Flights:** not included. The fare depends on the date and on who is travelling, children included, so it's quoted separately. This now appears:
  - as a panel row
  - under "Not included"
  - in "Worth knowing"
  - in the children's row ("50% off the tour price, flights quoted separately"), as on the Abu Simbel by plane page
- **Flight times:** outbound usually between 06:00 and 08:00, return usually around 21:00–22:00. These times appear in the panel and in the timeline. The old "about 12 hours" is gone, because the day now runs from the morning flight to the late return.
- **Luxor market:** kept as a stop "if time allows", before the airport, described as a lively cultural close to the day.
- **Entrance fees:** all included, standard tickets only. The Valley of the Kings visit uses the standard ticket (the tombs open on the day). Special tombs such as Tutankhamun's (KV62) aren't part of this tour, so the "Optional extras" block and the "add Tutankhamun's tomb" line are gone. "Make it your own" now suggests seeing his treasures at the GEM instead.
- **Age line:** "There's no minimum age" is removed under rule 9; the line about children and shade stays in "Make it your own".
- **URL:** unchanged, so there's no redirect.
