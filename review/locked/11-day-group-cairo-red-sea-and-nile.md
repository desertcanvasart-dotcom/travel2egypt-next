## Opening

The most spacious of our group journeys: a first day given to rest, an early pause by the Red Sea, and a full four-night Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan, with Abu Simbel and the oldest pyramids to close.

---

## Why this route works

It is built for a long arrival. The first day asks nothing of you, Cairo opens gently with the old and Islamic city before the big Giza day, and the Red Sea comes early, so the hardest stretch, four nights on the Nile with temples most days, falls after you have recovered. The group travels together on fixed dates with a shared, licensed Egyptologist guide.

## Your eleven days

### Day 1 · Cairo
**Arrival**

You’re met at Cairo airport, helped through arrival formalities and taken to the hotel. Nothing else is planned: the first day is for rest. Dinner is included if your arrival time allows.

**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Dinner (depending on arrival time)
**Travel:** Meet and greet at the airport · private transfer
**Pace:** Rest

---

### Day 2 · Cairo
**Old Cairo, the Citadel and Islamic Cairo**

Old Cairo, with some of Egypt’s oldest churches. The Citadel of Saladin and the Mosque of Muhammad Ali, whose alabaster interior and slim minarets crown the skyline. After lunch, El-Moez Street, one of the finest concentrations of medieval architecture in the Islamic world, and Khan el-Khalili, trading since the 14th century.

**The day:** Old Cairo · Citadel of Saladin · Mosque of Muhammad Ali · El-Moez Street · Khan el-Khalili
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers

---

### Day 3 · Cairo
**The Grand Egyptian Museum and the Pyramids of Giza**

The Grand Egyptian Museum, beside the Giza plateau and fully open since 1 November 2025, with the complete Tutankhamun collection: the gold mask, the nested coffins and the gilded shrines, together for the first time. Then the plateau itself: the Great Pyramid of Khufu, the pyramids of Khafre and Menkaure, and the Great Sphinx. Museum first, monument second, and no drive across the city between them.

**The day:** Grand Egyptian Museum · Giza pyramids · Great Sphinx
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfers

---

### Day 4 · Cairo → Hurghada
**To the Red Sea**

A short domestic flight to Hurghada and the transfer to your all-inclusive resort. The rest of the day is free for the beach and the pool: the deliberate change from the city to the coast.

**Overnight:** Red Sea resort, Hurghada, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · dinner
**Travel:** Domestic flight Cairo → Hurghada · private transfers
**Pace:** Easy

---

### Day 5 · Hurghada
**Recovery by the Red Sea**

A full free day by the sea. Use the beach and the resort, or take an optional snorkelling or diving trip over the reef, a boat trip or a desert excursion. Arriving rested at the Nile cruise makes the temple days that follow far better.

**The day:** your choice
**Overnight:** Red Sea resort, Hurghada, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** As much or as little as you like

---

### Day 6 · Hurghada → Luxor
**Across the desert, and aboard**

After breakfast, the drive across the Eastern Desert to Luxor, about three and a half to four hours, from the coast back into the Nile Valley. You board your Nile cruise ship, your home for the next four nights. Depending on the arrival time and the ship’s schedule, the first Luxor visits may begin this afternoon.

**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Luxor
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private air-conditioned vehicle, Hurghada → Luxor

---

### Day 7 · Luxor
**The temples and the tombs**

A full day on the cruise’s Luxor programme, which, depending on the sailing schedule, takes in the Valley of the Kings, the temple of Hatshepsut, the Colossi of Memnon, and the great temples of Karnak and Luxor on the east bank.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon · Karnak · Luxor Temple
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Luxor
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfers · by cruise

**Optional on this day:**
- The tombs of Tutankhamun, Seti I and Ramesses VI: extra tickets

---

### Day 8 · Luxor → Aswan
**Edfu, Kom Ombo and the sail south**

The ship sails south toward Aswan. The Temple of Horus at Edfu, built between 237 and 57 BC, its walls, roof and inner sanctuary intact. Kom Ombo, with two of every doorway and sanctuary for Sobek and Horus the Elder. Between the temples, the day belongs to the deck and the river.

**The day:** Temple of Horus at Edfu · Temple of Kom Ombo
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** By cruise

---

### Day 9 · Aswan
**Abu Simbel by air, and Aswan**

A short flight from Aswan to Abu Simbel, to the two rock-cut temples Ramesses II made for himself and his queen Nefertari, and back. Depending on timing, the day may also take in Philae, the temple of Isis on its island. If the flight times don’t line up, the visit is made by private road transfer from Aswan, longer but to the same temples. You return to the ship for the last night on board.

**The day:** Abu Simbel · Aswan
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Aswan
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Domestic flight to Abu Simbel and back, or private road transfer · private transfers

---

### Day 10 · Aswan → Cairo
**The oldest pyramids, and a farewell dinner**

After disembarking, the transfer to Aswan airport and the flight to Cairo, then south of the city to where pyramid-building began. Memphis, the first capital of a united Egypt. Saqqara, with the Step Pyramid of Djoser, around 2650 BC. Dahshur, where the Bent Pyramid and the Red Pyramid show the builders learning to raise a true pyramid. In the evening, a farewell dinner: on a Nile dinner cruise, or at the revolving restaurant of the Cairo Tower.

**The day:** Memphis · Saqqara · Step Pyramid · Dahshur · farewell dinner
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Domestic flight Aswan → Cairo · private transfers

---

### Day 11 · Cairo
**Departure**

Breakfast, check-out and the transfer to Cairo airport.

**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Private transfer to the airport
