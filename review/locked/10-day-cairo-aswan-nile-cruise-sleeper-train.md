## Opening

Ten days with the Nile through the middle of them: Giza and the old city in Cairo, a night train south, Aswan’s temple on its island, three nights on a cruise down to Luxor, both banks of ancient Thebes, and a flight back to Cairo to finish.

---

## Why this route works

Cairo comes first, while you are fresh for its scale. The train carries you south overnight, so no day is spent getting there. From Aswan the river does the work: it runs north to Luxor, past Kom Ombo and Edfu, and the temples arrive one at a time. The flight back leaves a last, easier day for Saqqara and a dinner on the water.

## Your ten days

### Day 1 · Cairo
**Arrival**

You’re met at Cairo airport and taken to your hotel. The rest of the day is yours to settle in before the programme begins.

**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Travel:** Meet and greet at the airport · private transfer
**Pace:** Easy

---

### Day 2 · Cairo
**The pyramids and the Egyptian Museum**

The Giza plateau first: the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC, and the Great Sphinx below them. After lunch, the Egyptian Museum on Tahrir Square, with its colossal statuary, the royal gold from Tanis and finds from across the country.

**The day:** Giza pyramids · Great Sphinx · Egyptian Museum
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers

**Optional on this day:**
- Inside one of the pyramids: extra ticket

---

### Day 3 · Cairo → Aswan
**The Citadel, the bazaar and the night train**

The Citadel, begun by Saladin in 1176, with the Mosque of Muhammad Ali inside its walls, then Khan El Khalili, a bazaar quarter that has traded since the 14th century. In the evening you board the sleeper train for Aswan.

**The day:** Saladin Citadel · Mosque of Muhammad Ali · Khan El Khalili
**Overnight:** Sleeper train
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner (on the train)
**Travel:** Private transfers · overnight train

---

### Day 4 · Aswan
**The High Dam, the Unfinished Obelisk and Philae**

You wake to Upper Egypt outside the window. The High Dam, completed in 1970, which created Lake Nasser. The Unfinished Obelisk, still lying in its granite quarry where a crack stopped the work. Then by boat to Philae, the temple of Isis, taken apart and rebuilt on Agilkia Island to save it from the water. Late in the day, a felucca sail past the islands.

**The day:** Aswan High Dam · Unfinished Obelisk · Temple of Philae · felucca
**Overnight:** Aswan hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast (on the train) · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers · motorboat to Philae · felucca

---

### Day 5 · Aswan
**Board your cruise**

You board your five-star cruise ship, with its pool deck. No sites are planned: after three full days, an afternoon on board is the point.

**Overnight:** Nile cruise, Aswan
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** Easy

**Optional on this day:**
- Abu Simbel before boarding, by road or by air, with your guide: extra

---

### Day 6 · Kom Ombo → Edfu
**Two temples from the river**

Kom Ombo, built in two matching halves for two gods, Sobek the crocodile and Horus the Elder. Then the temple of Horus at Edfu, built between 237 and 57 BC, with its walls, roof and sanctuary still standing.

**The day:** Kom Ombo · Temple of Horus at Edfu
**Overnight:** Nile cruise
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner

---

### Day 7 · Luxor
**Karnak and Luxor Temple**

Karnak, enlarged by one pharaoh after another over some fifteen centuries, with its Great Hypostyle Hall of 134 columns. Then Luxor Temple, joined to Karnak by the Avenue of Sphinxes, reopened in November 2021.

**The day:** Karnak · Great Hypostyle Hall · Luxor Temple · Avenue of Sphinxes
**Overnight:** Nile cruise, Luxor
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner

**Optional on this day:**
- Luxor Temple after dark, arriving by horse-drawn carriage: extra

---

### Day 8 · Luxor → Cairo
**The West Bank, then the flight north**

The Valley of the Kings, with more than sixty royal tombs cut into the limestone, Tutankhamun’s among them. The terraced temple of Hatshepsut at Deir el-Bahari, and the Colossi of Memnon, two seated figures of Amenhotep III. In the afternoon, the flight to Cairo.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers · domestic flight

**Optional on this day:**
- The tombs of Tutankhamun, Seti I and Ramesses VI: extra tickets
- Medinet Habu, the temple of Ramesses III: extra

---

### Day 9 · Cairo
**Memphis, Saqqara, Dahshur and dinner on the Nile**

Memphis, the first capital of a united Egypt, with its fallen colossus of Ramesses II. Saqqara, where Djoser’s Step Pyramid, built around 2650 BC, is the oldest large stone building in the world. Dahshur, where the Bent and Red Pyramids show builders working out the true pyramid. In the evening, a dinner cruise on the Nile.

**The day:** Memphis · Saqqara Step Pyramid · Dahshur · Nile dinner cruise
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfers

**Optional on this day:**
- The Grand Egyptian Museum, home of the Tutankhamun collection: extra
- Coptic Cairo, with the Hanging Church and Ben Ezra Synagogue: extra
- Alexandria for the day in place of the pyramid fields: extra

---

### Day 10 · Cairo
**Departure**

Breakfast, and your transfer to Cairo airport.

**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Private transfer to the airport
