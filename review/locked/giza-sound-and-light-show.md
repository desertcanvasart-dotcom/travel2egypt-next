## Opening

Darkness improves very few views; the Giza Plateau is one of them. At night, with the city’s clutter gone and the monuments lit from below, the pyramids become geometry against the stars. The Sound and Light Show isn’t polished theatre or a lecture: it’s a popular spectacle with real history in it, narrated by the Sphinx, in a setting that makes up for every imperfection.

---

## Why the day runs this way

The show runs most evenings and lasts about an hour, so the evening is built around its start time, with your transfers and time to be seated on top. Cairo’s traffic can be stubborn even after dark, and we allow for it in the pickup time. Plan dinner before or after, not during. And choose your side of the daytime visit: the night before, as an overture, or the night after, when you know what the lights are landing on.

## Your day

### Evening · The Giza Plateau

**Pickup from your hotel**
Your representative collects you in a private air-conditioned car for the drive out to the plateau.

**Your seat in front of the Sphinx**
You’re shown to the viewing area facing the Sphinx, about 73 metres long and cut from the bedrock under Khafre, with the three pyramids rising behind it. Translation headsets are available and included: pick one up before you sit down if the narration isn’t in your language.
*Sites:* Great Sphinx · Giza Plateau

**The show**
The Sphinx is the storyteller, a device that sounds kitsch but works, because the monument does the dramatic lifting. It recounts ancient Egypt’s history with the pyramids at its centre: how they were built, and the three kings who built them, Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure. Expect a theatrical overview rather than a lecture, with music, and coloured lights sweeping the monuments; clear nights are the most dramatic.

**Back to your hotel**
Your representative meets you after the show.

## Is this day for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- have seen the plateau by day and want it again under lights
- are travelling with children: the story is accessible and the walking is minimal
- have just arrived and want a first look at Egypt’s most famous landscape

**Worth knowing:**
- The production dates from 1961 in its first form and shows its age: the lighting rigs are visible and the script lurches at times. The setting is the point.
- The show is occasionally cancelled for maintenance or private events; we confirm your date.
- Desert evenings can be cool in winter; bring a layer.
