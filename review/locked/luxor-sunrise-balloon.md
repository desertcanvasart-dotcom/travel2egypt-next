## Opening

Luxor's West Bank from three to five hundred metres up. On the ground, the West Bank is a series of separate visits: Hatshepsut's temple, the Colossi of Memnon, the tomb valleys. From a balloon, it's one map. Go up at sunrise, as the cliffs above the Valley of the Kings catch the first light, or a little later, once the sun is up, on the after-sunrise flight. Either way, you land to a cup of tea or coffee and a flight certificate.

---

## Why the day runs this way

There are two flights, and they're priced differently. The **sunrise flight** is timed to first light: the inflation and the safety briefing happen in the dark, and pickup is well before dawn. The **after-sunrise flight** lifts off a little later in the morning, once the sun is up, for a slightly gentler start. You choose one when you book, and the price follows your choice.

Either way, the wind decides the rest. The balloon drifts north-east or south depending on the morning, and the weather can postpone a flight on the day itself. That's why it works best early in your Luxor stay, with a spare morning behind it, and before your West Bank day, so the ground visits come with the map already in your head.

## Your day

### Morning · The West Bank from the air

**Pickup from your hotel or boat**
Early in the morning; before dawn for the sunrise flight. A small ferry takes you across the Nile to the launch field on the West Bank, where the ground crew is already inflating the balloon.

**Briefing and launch**
The pilot gives the safety briefing while the basket is held to the ground. A minute or two after lift-off, you're at flying height, usually between 300 and 500 metres, depending on the wind.

**The flight**
The first thing to come clear is the sharp line between the green cultivated land and the desert. Then, depending on the wind: the Colossi of Memnon out in the fields, the three colonnaded terraces of Hatshepsut's temple against the cliff, the dry valley behind it leading towards the Valley of the Kings, and across the river, the Nile and Karnak. The pilot may climb to widen the view, or drop low over the fields.
*Sites (seen from the air):* Colossi of Memnon · Temple of Hatshepsut · Valley of the Kings · Karnak

**Landing**
You land on farmland, with the chase vehicles already waiting. Tea or coffee on the ground, and a certificate to remember the flight.

**Back to your hotel or boat**
A short ferry brings you back to the East Bank, and a transfer takes you to your hotel or boat, with the whole day ahead.
