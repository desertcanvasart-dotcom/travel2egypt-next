## Opening

An airport transfer isn’t glamorous, but a bad one, with a missing driver or a confusing visa queue, can sour the first hours of a trip. This is a private car between Sharm El Sheikh Airport and your hotel: a representative meets you in arrivals, an air-conditioned car takes you to the coast, and on your last day the same car takes you back. Book one direction or both; the price is the same each way.

---

## Why the day runs this way

The drive is short. Sharm El Sheikh’s hotels spread along the coast from Nabq, close to the airport, south through Shark's Bay and Naama Bay to Sharm El Maya, and most transfers take twenty to forty-five minutes. The road drops from the bare Sinai plateau to the Red Sea, and the change from desert to turquoise water is a fine first impression. We follow your flight on arrival, and set the pickup time with you on departure.

## Your day

### Arrival · Airport to your hotel

**Visa and immigration**
If you need a visa on arrival, buy it at the bank counter inside the terminal, before the immigration desks. Have cash ready; cards aren’t always accepted. Place the visa sticker on a blank page of your passport, then queue for the arrival seal. Late-night arrivals in the peak Red Sea season can be slow. With meet and assist, we meet you at the gate and help you through.

**Baggage**
Check the screens for your belt number. If a bag doesn’t appear, find the airline’s handling agent before you leave the hall: a delayed bag is much easier to resolve from inside than from your hotel.

**Meeting your representative**
Beyond the exit gate, your representative waits in the arrivals hall with a sign bearing the Travel2Egypt name and your own. The exit can be busy with other drivers; look for the sign rather than answering approaches.

**The drive to your hotel**
A private air-conditioned car, not a shared shuttle, through the outskirts of town to your coastal district.

### Departure · Hotel to the airport

**Pickup at your hotel**
Your driver collects you at the time we agreed and loads your bags.

**Drop-off at the terminal**
Your driver takes you to the terminal door. With meet and assist, someone helps you through to check-in.
