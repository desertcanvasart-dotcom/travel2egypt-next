## Opening

The road north from Aswan follows the Nile for most of its 215 kilometres, through sugar cane and villages, with the river slipping in and out of view. Two temples stand right on the way, Kom Ombo and Edfu, and both deserve the stop. This private day takes you from Aswan to Luxor door to door, with your own car, driver and Egyptologist, the temples on the way, and lunch when you arrive.

---

## Why the day runs this way

An early start leaves room for both temples without arriving in Luxor late. Kom Ombo comes first, about 45 kilometres north, best in the early light by the water; Edfu is 60 kilometres further, and then it’s 115 kilometres to Luxor. Lunch waits until you arrive, so the temples aren’t rushed, and you’re dropped at your hotel, ship or the airport afterwards. Give us the time you need to be in Luxor, and we’ll set the departure.

## Your day

### Morning · Kom Ombo and Edfu

**Pickup in Aswan**
Your driver and Egyptologist collect you from your hotel or cruise ship in a private air-conditioned car.

**The Temple of Kom Ombo**
Built under the Ptolemies, mostly in the 2nd and 1st centuries BC, for two gods at once, Sobek the crocodile and Horus the Elder, with two parallel axes from the pylons to twin sanctuaries. In the Crocodile Museum next door, mummified crocodiles from nearby catacombs bring the Sobek cult to life.
*Sites:* Temple of Kom Ombo · Crocodile Museum

**The Temple of Horus, Edfu**
Begun under Ptolemy III in 237 BC and finished some 180 years later, then buried under sand and silt, which is why it came out of the 19th-century excavations almost whole: 36-metre pylons, roofed halls, and the granite shrine still in the sanctuary. After the ruins of Karnak, it shows what a complete temple looked like. Your guide reads the Horus myth on its walls with you. Allow at least an hour.
*Sites:* Temple of Horus, Edfu
