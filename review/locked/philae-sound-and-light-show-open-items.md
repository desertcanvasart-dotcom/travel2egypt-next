  - A panel row reads "Winter evenings: genuinely cold on the water. Bring a warm jacket".
  - The first, bold item in "Worth knowing": from October to March, evenings on the water and the open island are genuinely cold, colder than most visitors expect in Egypt. Bring a warm jacket, and a scarf if you feel the cold.
- **No meal:** a line in "Worth knowing" says there's no meal on this evening, only the boat, the temple and the show. Dinner and drinks stay under "Not included".
