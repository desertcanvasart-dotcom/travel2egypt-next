## Opening

This is a Red Sea holiday, not a pharaonic tour with a beach attached. Six nights at Sharm El Sheikh, at the southern tip of the Sinai, where the Gulfs of Aqaba and Suez meet and the desert drops straight into the reef. Then a short flight to Cairo for a felucca and a dinner cruise on the Nile before you fly home.

---

## Why this route works

Most of the week is left open on purpose, so the choice between the reef, the desert interior and doing nothing is made on the ground, not in advance. Ras Mohammed, the Tiran reefs, Mount Sinai and the Coloured Canyon are all within a day of the resort, and you add the ones you want. Cairo comes last, for one evening on the river, so the trip ends on the Nile.

You’re met at Sharm El Sheikh airport, whether you fly in direct or through Cairo, and taken to your resort: a short drive. The rest of the day is a first look at the water and the evening light over the Gulf, the desert hills going from red to violet behind the coast.

### Day 2 · Sharm El Sheikh
**The reef from the shore**

The reef begins almost at the shoreline here, so no boat is needed to reach it. Snorkel over the coral directly off the beach, take a guided dive if you’re certified, or a glass-bottom boat if you’d rather stay dry. Windsurfing and sailing use the Gulf’s steady northerly breeze, and the pools and beach are equally legitimate. Day two is for seeing why this coast has the reputation it does, and deciding how much more of it you want.

**The day:** Your choice
**Overnight:** Sharm El Sheikh resort, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** As much or as little as you like

### Day 3 · Sharm El Sheikh
**Ras Mohammed**

At the very tip of the peninsula, Ras Mohammed has been protected since 1983: reef walls that drop sheer into deep water, where the serious diving is, and shallower reefs for snorkellers. A full day by boat or by road from the resort, for those who want it; for those who don’t, another day on the beach.

**The day:** Your choice
**Overnight:** Sharm El Sheikh resort, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** As much or as little as you like

**Optional on this day:**
- A day at Ras Mohammed National Park, by boat or by road: extra

### Day 4 · Sharm El Sheikh
**The Strait of Tiran, Naama Bay and the Old Market**

Where the Gulf of Aqaba narrows, four reefs are strung along the Strait of Tiran, reached by day boat from Naama Bay, the strip of hotels and dive centres where most boat trips leave. In the evening, the Old Market, the original Sharm before the resort town grew around it, about ten minutes from Naama Bay: spice sellers, textiles and seafood along the waterfront.

**The day:** Your choice
**Overnight:** Sharm El Sheikh resort, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** As much or as little as you like

**Optional on this day:**
- A snorkelling boat to the Tiran reefs: extra
- A semi-submarine or parasailing over the bay: extra

### Day 5 · Sharm El Sheikh
**Inland: the Sinai mountains**

The Sinai is not only coast. Inland it is a granite mountain desert, peaks above 2,000 metres and a silence the coast doesn’t have. The main excursion is Mount Sinai and Saint Catherine’s Monastery: you leave in the night, climb in the dark to the 2,285-metre summit for sunrise, and come down to the 6th-century monastery at its foot. Or the Coloured Canyon north of Nuweiba, a narrow sandstone slot in ochre, purple and white; or Dahab, about an hour up the coast.

**The day:** Your choice
**Overnight:** Sharm El Sheikh resort, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** As much or as little as you like

**Optional on this day:**
- Mount Sinai at sunrise and Saint Catherine’s Monastery, leaving in the night: extra
- The Coloured Canyon, or a day in Dahab: extra

### Day 6 · Sharm El Sheikh
**A day without a programme**

A week on the Red Sea needs at least one day that asks for nothing, and this is it, placed after the mountain for anyone who climbed in the night. Go back to the reef, pick up an excursion you missed, or stay inside the resort all day. Rest isn’t a gap in the itinerary; it is part of why a week on this coast works.

**Overnight:** Sharm El Sheikh resort, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** Easy

### Day 7 · Sharm El Sheikh → Cairo
**A felucca and a dinner cruise on the Nile**

After breakfast, the short flight to Cairo. In the late afternoon, a felucca on the Nile, the wooden sailing boat that has worked this river for centuries, moving slowly with the city on both banks. The evening ends with a dinner cruise, the lit skyline of Cairo sliding past. After a week on the sea, the Nile comes back into the trip without hurrying it.

**The day:** Felucca on the Nile · dinner cruise
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast (at the resort) · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfer to Sharm El Sheikh airport · domestic flight Sharm El Sheikh → Cairo (not included) · private transfers · felucca
