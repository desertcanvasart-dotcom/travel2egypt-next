## Opening

There’s nothing romantic about a bad arrival, or a rushed departure. This private transfer solves both, nothing more, nothing less: a representative meets you when you land and drives you to your hotel, and on your last day takes you back to the airport. Luxor’s airport is small and easy, and with someone waiting for you, both ends of your stay are simple. Book one direction or both; the price is the same each way.

---

## Why the day runs this way

We follow your flight, so on arrival the car is waiting when you come out, however long the visa queue or the baggage belt takes. The drive is short: fifteen to twenty minutes to the East Bank hotels in normal traffic, a little longer to the West Bank or the edge of town. On departure, we set the pickup time with you around your flight.

## Your day

### Arrival · Airport to your hotel

**Visa and immigration**
If you need a visa on arrival, buy the stamp at one of the bank counters in the terminal, fix it into a blank page of your passport, then go to the immigration desk. In peak season, allow an extra fifteen to twenty minutes. With meet and assist, we meet you at the gate and help you through.

**Your luggage**
If a bag is delayed or missing, report it at the airline desk before you leave the baggage hall. Your representative will wait.

**Meeting your representative**
Just beyond the exit, in the arrivals hall, look for a sign with Travel2Egypt and your name. If you can’t see it, stay in the terminal and call the number in your booking confirmation.

**The drive to your hotel**
A private air-conditioned car, not shared; the driver handles your bags. On the way, the Theban hills show on the western horizon: your first glimpse of what you came for.

### Departure · Hotel to the airport

**Pickup at your hotel**
Your driver collects you at the time we agreed, on the East or West Bank, and loads your bags.

**Drop-off at the terminal**
Luxor’s airport has a single terminal; your driver takes you to the door. With meet and assist, someone helps you through to check-in.
