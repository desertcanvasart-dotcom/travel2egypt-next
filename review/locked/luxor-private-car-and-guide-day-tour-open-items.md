# Private Car & Guide in Luxor: open items

Source: the live page `/luxor-private-car-and-guide`, read 17 Nov 2026. Owner answers received 22 Nov 2026.

## Resolved (22 Nov), applied in all three languages

- **Same model as the other cities:** a private car and driver plus an Egyptologist guide in the guest's language, for up to 8 hours, visiting whatever the guest chooses. The approved "Choose your stops" format is used.
- **What's included:**
  - car and driver
  - guide
  - driver's tip
  - one bottle of water per person
- **What's not included:**
  - lunch: it's been removed from the panel, the timeline and the inclusions. This follows the Cairo and Aswan car-and-guide products, as an exception to rule 6.
  - entrance tickets, drinks and airport transfers
- **One crossing of the river:** guests visit each bank once and don't go back and forth. They choose which bank to start on. At the end of the day they're taken back to their hotel or ship, on whichever side it is. This appears in the panel, in "Why the day runs this way", in "Worth knowing" and in "Make it your own".
- **Pickup:** from the guest's hotel or cruise ship, on the East or the West Bank. Pickup zones and their prices come from Autoura at booking. That's how a West Bank guest who starts on the East Bank gets the right price and the right return.
- **Age:** the "no minimum age" line has been removed. The standard "Children under 12: 50% off" row has been added. The stamina note for younger children stays.
- **URL:** unchanged. No redirect.
