## Why the day runs this way

Cairo is big, and the distances are real: the Giza Plateau is about 20 kilometres from Islamic Cairo. Try to fit everything into one day and you'll see all of it shallowly. So the day is built on three sites, done properly, plus the bazaar if you want it. The pyramids, the Citadel and Coptic Cairo make a good day; so do the pyramids, a museum and the Citadel. Your guide helps you choose the order around the opening times, the crowds and the traffic, so the three fit comfortably within eight hours.

## Your day

### Morning · Pickup

**Pickup from your hotel**
Your driver and Egyptologist collect you in a private air-conditioned car.

### Choose your stops: any 3

**The Grand Egyptian Museum**
At the foot of the Giza Plateau, with the complete Tutankhamun collection, more than 5,000 objects from his tomb, alongside statuary and royal regalia. Allow at least two hours; three if you want to read the labels.
*Sites:* Grand Egyptian Museum

**The Egyptian Museum, Tahrir Square**
The old building has the feel of a cabinet of curiosities, and an intimacy the new museum can't copy. If you haven't been to either museum, pick one for a single day: both is too much. The royal mummies are at the NMEC, a separate visit.
*Sites:* Egyptian Museum

**The pyramids and the Sphinx**
Khufu's Great Pyramid and the Sphinx, cut from the bedrock, probably under Khafre. The plateau is busy in the mornings; your guide knows the angles worth walking to, and which pyramid interiors are open that day.
*Sites:* Giza Plateau · Great Sphinx

**The Citadel and the Mosque of Muhammad Ali**
On a limestone spur above the city, with views to Giza on a clear day. Saladin founded the Citadel; Muhammad Ali's alabaster-clad mosque crowns it. Shoulders and knees covered, shoes off at the door.
*Sites:* Citadel of Saladin · Mosque of Muhammad Ali

**Al-Muizz Street**
The spine of Fatimid Cairo, partly pedestrianised, with the Madrasa and Mausoleum of Barquq, the Mosque of al-Hakim and the Sabil-Kuttab of Abd al-Rahman Katkhuda along the way.
*Sites:* Al-Muizz Street

**Coptic Cairo**
Compact and quiet: the Hanging Church above the Roman gatehouse of Babylon, the church of Saints Sergius and Bacchus, and the Ben Ezra Synagogue, where the Cairo Geniza was found.
*Sites:* Hanging Church · Abu Serga · Ben Ezra Synagogue

### Optional · Khan El Khalili

**The bazaar, if you'd like**
On top of your three sites, you can finish in Khan El Khalili. Your guide can steer you past the pushiest stalls to the workshops most visitors miss.
*Sites:* Khan El Khalili

**Back to your hotel**
Within your eight hours. If you want to stay out longer, extra hours are charged separately.
