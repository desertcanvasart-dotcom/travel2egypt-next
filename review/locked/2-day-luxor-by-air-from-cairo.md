## Opening

Few places pack as much ancient achievement into a few kilometres as Luxor. This private two-day trip flies you down from Cairo for Karnak and Luxor Temple on the first day and the West Bank tombs on the second, then flies you back.

---

## Why this route works

The flight takes about an hour. The alternatives, a night on the sleeper train or a long day on the road each way, use up exactly the energy you need for walking big, sun-exposed sites. Flying saves the day for the sites, and a night in Luxor leaves you rested for the tombs.

## Your two days

### Day 1 · Cairo → Luxor
**Karnak and Luxor Temple**

An early pickup from your Cairo hotel and the flight south. Your guide and car meet you in Luxor and go straight to work. Karnak first: some 100 hectares built over about 1,500 years for Amun, Mut and Khonsu, with the Great Hypostyle Hall of 134 columns up to 21 metres high, the Sacred Lake and Hatshepsut’s standing obelisk. It needs time. Then Luxor Temple, about three kilometres south along the Avenue of Sphinxes: begun by Amenhotep III around 1390 BC and enlarged by Ramses II, with seated colossi and a surviving obelisk at the gate; its twin has stood in Paris since 1836. Lunch at a local restaurant, then check in at your hotel. The evening is free.

**The day:** Karnak · Great Hypostyle Hall · Sacred Lake · Avenue of Sphinxes · Luxor Temple
**Overnight:** Jolie Ville Kings Island Resort, Luxor
**Meals:** Lunch
**Travel:** Pickup from your Cairo hotel · domestic flight Cairo → Luxor · private air-conditioned car

**Optional on this day:**
- The White Chapel of Senusret I, in Karnak’s Open-Air Museum: extra ticket
- Luxor Temple lit after dark, and a walk on the Corniche at dusk

### Day 2 · Luxor → Cairo
**The West Bank, then the flight home**

Breakfast at your hotel, then across to the West Bank. The Valley of the Kings, the New Kingdom royal tombs cut into the Theban cliffs, where your ticket includes three of the standard tombs. Then the terraced temple of Hatshepsut at Deir el-Bahari, and the Colossi of Memnon, two seated figures of Amenhotep III standing since about 1350 BC. Lunch at a local restaurant. In the afternoon or early evening, the transfer to Luxor airport and the flight back to Cairo, where a driver takes you to your hotel.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings (three standard tombs) · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon
**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private air-conditioned car · domestic flight Luxor → Cairo · transfer to your Cairo hotel

**Optional on this day:**
- The tomb of Tutankhamun (KV62): extra ticket
