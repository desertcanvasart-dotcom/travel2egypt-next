## Opening

About 215 kilometres south along the Nile Valley, in about four hours: your own car and driver, from your hotel in Luxor to your hotel in Aswan. This is a transfer and nothing more. No guide, no temples, no tickets; just the move between the two river cities, done comfortably and on your timetable.

---

## Why the day runs this way

It’s a daytime drive, leaving Luxor in the morning and arriving in Aswan around midday, with the afternoon still yours. You choose the departure time, and your driver is at your hotel door when you’re ready. There’s no station or airport at either end, and your luggage goes in the car with you. Tell us your two hotels and the hour you’d like to leave, and we’ll arrange the car and the road permit around it.

### Morning · Luxor to Aswan

### Midday · Arrival in Aswan

**Drop-off at your hotel**
Your driver delivers you and your luggage to your hotel in Aswan, around midday.

## Is this day for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- are moving from Luxor to Aswan with your hotel already booked
- are a family, or have a lot of luggage
- would rather look out of the window than manage a hire car or a station

**Worth knowing:**
- It’s a transfer only, with no stops at the temples on the way.
- Four hours is four hours: bring something to read, or plan to sleep.
- The car is air-conditioned, which matters: the south is hot for much of the year.
