## Opening

This is a journey for watching, not for ticking things off. For three days a private two-cabin boat takes you around the bays and rocky islands of Lake Nasser, setting out from near Abu Simbel, with guided walks where the water meets the desert. Dorcas gazelles and desert foxes live here, but they don’t appear on demand. The days are built around the waterbirds, the tracks in the sand and the landscape itself.

---

## Why this route works

Sleeping aboard puts you on the lake at the hours that matter: early mornings, when the light is soft and the desert cooler, and evenings at anchor under an open sky. The pace is relaxed in between, with rest on the shaded deck through the middle of the day. Two guides share the work: a birdwatching naturalist who reads the birds and the tracks, and a local boatman who knows the lake. The anchorages and landing sites follow an agreed circuit, chosen each day according to permissions, weather, water levels and what the birds are doing.

## Your three days

### Day 1 · Aswan → Abu Simbel → Lake Nasser
**Birds, shores and sunset**

**Morning.** Pickup from your hotel in Aswan at 07:30 and the drive south to Abu Simbel, about three hours. First, a visit to the two temples of Abu Simbel, Ramesses II’s Great Temple and the temple of Nefertari, moved here in the 1960s to save them from the rising lake. Then, near the temple, you board the boat, meet your guides and crew, settle into your cabin and get a briefing, then set out across the lake. On the way to the first anchorage, your naturalist helps you look for herons, egrets, cormorants, kingfishers and other waterbirds around the sheltered bays and rocky islands, depending on the route.

**Afternoon.** Lunch aboard, then time to rest on the shaded deck. Later, you step ashore at an approved landing site for a guided desert walk where the lake meets the desert: drought-adapted plants, rock formations and animal tracks in the sand. The pace is gentle, with stops for observation and photography.

**Evening.** Back aboard around sunset. Dinner is freshly prepared by the crew, and the evening is for the stars.

**The day:** the drive to Abu Simbel · the temples of Abu Simbel · embarkation · birdwatching from the boat · guided desert walk · sunset at anchor
**Overnight:** private two-cabin boat on Lake Nasser
**Meals:** Lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Pickup from your Aswan hotel · private car, Aswan → Abu Simbel · private boat
**Pace:** A morning on the road and at the temples, then an easy afternoon on the lake

### Day 2 · On Lake Nasser
**Dawn tracking and the dunes**

**Early morning.** An early start, while the light is soft and the desert cooler. A short boat transfer takes you to the landing site chosen for the morning’s walk. Your naturalist leads you through shoreline and wadi habitats, stopping to read tracks and scan the landscape. Where recent local sightings support it, you search for Dorcas gazelles and desert foxes. They are wild animals in a vast landscape: you may see them, find signs that they’ve passed, or see neither.

**Morning.** Breakfast aboard, then more of the lake. Quiet bays give further chances for birdwatching and photography, with the route adjusted to the conditions and the birds present.

**Afternoon.** Lunch, and a rest aboard. In the late afternoon you go ashore to explore the dunes on foot: sandy slopes and the desert around them, how the wind shapes the land and how small animals leave traces of their movements. You pick a viewpoint to watch the colours change, then return to the boat.

**Evening.** Dinner aboard, and a second night on the lake.

**The day:** dawn walk and wildlife tracking · birdwatching in the bays · dune walk at sunset
**Overnight:** private two-cabin boat on Lake Nasser
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private boat · short boat transfers to landing sites · on foot
**Pace:** An early start, a quiet middle of the day, a walk in the late afternoon

### Day 3 · Lake Nasser → Abu Simbel → Aswan
**Morning birdwatching and the return**

**Early morning.** A last birdwatching outing through sheltered inlets and shoreline habitats while the lake is quiet. Your naturalist helps you identify what you see, and explains how each species uses the lake’s different environments.

**Morning.** Breakfast, then the cruise back towards Abu Simbel. If the route and conditions allow, one last short walk ashore or a stop for photographs.

**Midday.** Lunch aboard, then you disembark near Abu Simbel and drive back to your hotel in Aswan, about three hours.

**The day:** birdwatching in the inlets · the return cruise · the drive to Aswan
**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private boat · private car, Abu Simbel → your Aswan hotel
**Pace:** An early outing, then the return by boat and road

## Is this journey for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- want birds, quiet and open landscape more than monuments
- enjoy early mornings and walking at an unhurried pace
- take photographs

**Worth knowing:**
- **Sightings can’t be guaranteed.** Birdlife varies with the season and the habitat, and gazelles, foxes and other wildlife may not appear. Your guides put quiet observation first, and don’t disturb animals or approach dens and nesting sites.
- The journey runs from October to April.
- Your guides speak English. A guide in your own language can be added at an extra cost, which depends on the language; ask when you book.
- About three hours’ drive between Aswan and Abu Simbel at each end of the journey.
- The dunes are explored on foot. There’s no vehicle-based desert excursion.
- Early starts, and walks over sand, stones and uneven ground. Distances can be adapted to your group, but getting ashore needs reasonable mobility.
- Bring binoculars, comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, a refillable water bottle and a warm layer for the cool mornings and evenings.
- The anchorages and landing sites depend on permissions, weather, water levels and local conditions on the day.
