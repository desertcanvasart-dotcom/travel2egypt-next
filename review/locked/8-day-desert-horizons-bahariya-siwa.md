## Opening

A private two-oasis journey into Egypt’s deep west: the chalk formations of the White Desert, the dune chains of the Great Sand Sea, and Siwa, the Berber oasis where Alexander the Great was hailed as the son of Amun. On the way home, a night on the Mediterranean and the war cemeteries of El Alamein.

---

## Why this route works

It runs west in two stages. Three days for Bahariya and the deserts around it, landscapes that need no history to register; then the long road to Siwa, where the story thickens, with the Oracle, Berber culture and Roman traces. The pace is honest: long drives and a desert that isn’t comfortable in any usual sense, in exchange for space, silence and real distance from the city. The eighth day exists to make the way home humane: instead of one drive of 750 kilometres, the road is split by a night at Marsa Matruh, and the last stop is El Alamein.

## Your eight days

### Day 1 · Cairo → Bahariya
**The first oasis**

After breakfast, about four hours southwest to Bahariya, green with palms and olives in its depression, with hot springs and the small town of Bawiti at its heart. Lunch on the road, arrival in the late afternoon, and time to settle before dinner. A transition day, taken at that pace.

**Overnight:** Bahariya hotel
**Meals:** Lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private car, Cairo → Bahariya
**Pace:** Easy

---

### Day 2 · The Black Mountains → the White Desert
**Basalt hills, a salt lake, and a night under the formations**

An early start by 4×4 to the black, basalt-capped hills north of Bawiti, past the crumbling English House, then the dunes and the dazzling flat of the Salt Lake. In the afternoon, south to the White Desert National Park: chalk carved by the wind into Mushroom Rock, towers and columns, long shadows at dusk and a glow under a full moon. Camp among them, after everyone else has gone.

**The day:** Black Mountains · English House · Salt Lake · White Desert National Park
**Overnight:** Camping in the White Desert
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private 4×4

---

### Day 3 · Crystal Mountain, Agabat and the Black Desert → Bahariya
**The formations most visitors miss**

Crystal Mountain, a small outcrop of crystals that catches the light; the Agabat valley, its pale spires and arches best walked rather than photographed from the road; the Black Desert proper, the dark volcanic field; and El Hayz, a small settlement with a hot spring. Back to Bahariya for a last oasis night.

**The day:** Crystal Mountain · Agabat · Black Desert · El Hayz
**Overnight:** Bahariya hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private 4×4

---

### Day 4 · Bahariya → Siwa
**The long road west**

The longest drive of the trip into the oasis: 402 kilometres, six to seven hours through the northern desert as the land flattens and empties. Lunch on the way. Then the palm groves and grey escarpment of Siwa, fed by hundreds of freshwater springs, and dinner in the oasis. The permit for the road, which foreign visitors need, is arranged for you.

**Overnight:** Siwa hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private car, Bahariya → Siwa
**Pace:** A very long drive

---

### Day 5 · Siwa
**Shali, the Oracle and Cleopatra’s Spring**

**Morning.** The Shali Fortress, Siwa’s old town of salt-mud karshif on its rock, mostly dissolved by three days of rain in 1926 but still walkable. Then Aghurmi and the Temple of the Oracle of Amun, the sanctuary that drew Alexander across the desert, and the single carved wall of the temple at Umm Ubayda.

**Afternoon.** Cleopatra’s Spring, a stone-lined pool of cold, clear water, and sunset at Fatnas Island on its salt lake.

**The day:** Shali Fortress · Temple of the Oracle · Umm Ubayda · Cleopatra’s Spring · Fatnas Island
**Overnight:** Siwa hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private car

---

### Day 6 · The Great Sand Sea
**Fossils, springs and sandboarding**

South and west by 4×4 from Maraki village into the Great Sand Sea, its dune chains running in parallel for hundreds of kilometres, to rocks full of fossil shells and coral from a sea that covered the area some 20 to 40 million years ago. A swim in the cold spring of Shiata Lake, sandboarding on the dunes, and Bir Wahed, a warm, shallow spring with nothing in sight in any direction. Back to Siwa in the early evening.

**The day:** Maraki · Great Sand Sea · fossil beds · Shiata Lake · sandboarding · Bir Wahed
**Overnight:** Siwa hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private 4×4
**Pace:** The most active day

---

### Day 7 · Siwa → Marsa Matruh
**The Mountain of the Dead, then the road to the sea**

The Mountain of the Dead, with tombs cut in the 26th Dynasty and the Ptolemaic period: Si-Amun’s, with its Greek-Egyptian paintings, Mesu-Isis’s, and the Crocodile Tomb. Then Dakrour Mountain, and Abu Shrouf village with its old spring. Lunch in Siwa, then north across the desert to the Mediterranean coast, about four to four and a half hours. You arrive at Marsa Matruh in the afternoon, in time for the sea and dinner at the hotel.

**The day:** Mountain of the Dead · Dakrour Mountain · Abu Shrouf
**Overnight:** Marsa Matruh, Triumph White Sands Hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private car, Siwa → Marsa Matruh
**Pace:** A long drive

---

### Day 8 · Marsa Matruh → El Alamein → Cairo
**The war cemeteries, and the road home**

After breakfast, east along the coast to El Alamein, where in 1942 the Allied and Axis armies fought the battles that turned the war in North Africa. You visit the war cemeteries and memorials, Commonwealth, German and Italian, and the El Alamein museum. Lunch is included. Then the road to Cairo, arriving in the evening. Dinner is not included today.

**The day:** Commonwealth war cemetery · German memorial · Italian memorial · El Alamein museum
**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private car, Marsa Matruh → El Alamein → Cairo
**Pace:** A long drive
