## Opening

Five days on the Nile, sailing south from Luxor to Aswan, with your own Egyptologist at every site. Luxor’s two banks come first, then the river temples of Edfu and Kom Ombo, then Aswan, with Philae, the High Dam and a felucca. It is the classic river route, at a pace that leaves each day one place to take in.

---

## Why this route works

Starting in Luxor gives its monuments two full days while you are fresh: the temples of the living on the East Bank, the tombs of the dead on the West. The ship then carries you south past the two river temples, and Aswan, quieter and greener, comes last, when a slower day suits the end of the trip.

## Your five days

> **Your ship sets the exact order.** Cruise ships between Luxor and Aswan follow slightly different sailing schedules, and some visit Edfu and Kom Ombo on different days from those shown below. The sites are the same either way, and your final itinerary confirms the order for your ship.

### Day 1 · Luxor
**Board the cruise: Karnak, and Luxor Temple after dark**

You’re met at Luxor airport and taken to the ship for lunch. In the afternoon, Karnak, a precinct built and extended over some 1,500 years, with the Great Hypostyle Hall of 134 columns, the tallest 21 metres high. As the light goes, a horse-drawn carriage takes you to Luxor Temple, at the end of the Avenue of Sphinxes reopened in November 2021, to see it lit after dark.

**The day:** Karnak · Great Hypostyle Hall · Avenue of Sphinxes · Luxor Temple by night
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Luxor
**Meals:** Lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Meet and assist at Luxor airport · private transfers · horse-drawn carriage
**Pace:** An arrival, then a long afternoon and evening

### Day 2 · Luxor · the West Bank
**The Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut and the Colossi**

The Valley of the Kings, where the New Kingdom pharaohs cut their tombs into the limestone from around 1539 BC. More than sixty tombs have been found; your ticket includes three of the standard ones. Then Hatshepsut’s temple at Deir el-Bahari, three colonnaded terraces against the cliff, and the Colossi of Memnon, two seated figures of Amenhotep III about 18 metres tall. Back on board, the ship sails south.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings (three tombs) · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, sailing south
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfers · by cruise
**Pace:** An early start, then the river

**Optional on this day:**
- The tombs of Tutankhamun, Seti I and Ramses VI: extra tickets, one each

### Day 3 · Edfu and Kom Ombo
**Two river temples**

Edfu’s Temple of Horus, built between 237 and 57 BC, its pylon, enclosure wall, roof and sanctuary still standing, so you walk from the open court into ever darker inner rooms much as the priests did. In the afternoon, Kom Ombo, its plan doubled for two gods, Sobek the crocodile and Horus the Elder, with two entrances, two halls and two sanctuaries. The museum beside it holds mummified crocodiles from the site. The ship sails on to Aswan overnight.

**The day:** Temple of Horus at Edfu · Temple of Kom Ombo · Crocodile Museum
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, sailing to Aswan
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** By cruise · horse-drawn carriage at Edfu
**Pace:** Two temple stops, then the river

### Day 4 · Aswan
**Philae, the High Dam, the Unfinished Obelisk and a felucca**

By motorboat to Philae, the Temple of Isis, moved to Agilkia Island when the lake rose, in work completed in 1980. Then the Aswan High Dam, completed in 1970, which ended the annual flood and created Lake Nasser, and the Unfinished Obelisk, abandoned in its granite quarry when it cracked; finished, it would have stood about 42 metres tall. In the late afternoon, a felucca around Kitchener’s Island.

**The day:** Temple of Philae · Aswan High Dam · Unfinished Obelisk · felucca around Kitchener’s Island
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Aswan
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfers · motorboat to Philae · felucca
**Pace:** Aswan’s main sites in one day, with the outdoor visits in the cooler hours

**Optional on this day:**
- Philae Sound and Light Show in the evening: optional extra
