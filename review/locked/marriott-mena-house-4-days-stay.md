## Opening

Plenty of hotels in Giza can see the pyramids. The Marriott Mena House sits at the foot of the plateau, close enough to walk to it. This short private stay is built around that position: the pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum on your doorstep, one long day on the Mediterranean in Alexandria, and the rest of the time at the hotel.

---

## Why this route works

The hotel and the two great Giza sights stand within a few minutes of each other, so the Cairo day loses no time to the city's traffic. That leaves the energy for the one long drive of the trip, north to Alexandria, a Greek and Roman city that looks nothing like the Nile Valley. Arrival and departure are kept light, because the hotel is part of the reason you came.

## Your four days

### Day 1 · Giza
**Arrival at the Mena House**

You're met at Cairo airport and driven to the Marriott Mena House. There's no programme today. The hotel began in the 1860s as a hunting lodge for the Khedive Ismail and has been a hotel since the 1880s, in gardens below the plateau. Check in, find the view from the terrace, and if you have the energy, walk to the base of the pyramids in the late light.

**Overnight:** Marriott Mena House, Giza
**Travel:** Meet and greet at the airport, private transfer to the hotel
**Pace:** Easy

---

### Day 2 · Giza
**The pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum**

**Morning.** The Giza plateau, minutes from the hotel: the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC. You go inside the pyramid of Khafre or Menkaure, subject to site rules and availability on the day; a narrow descending passage leads to the burial chamber. Then the panorama viewpoint, where the three pyramids line up in one frame, and the Great Sphinx and the Valley Temple below.

**Midday.** Lunch at a local restaurant.

**Afternoon.** The Grand Egyptian Museum, at the edge of the plateau, with Tutankhamun's burial equipment shown together, from the golden throne to the shrines. Then back to the hotel for the evening.

**The day:** Giza pyramids · inside Khafre or Menkaure · panorama viewpoint · Great Sphinx · Valley Temple · Grand Egyptian Museum · Tutankhamun galleries
**Overnight:** Marriott Mena House, Giza
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers

**Optional on this day:**
- Inside the Great Pyramid of Khufu: extra ticket, subject to availability and site rules on the day
- A camel ride on the plateau: extra cost. We work with handlers we know, whose animals are in good condition, and the ride is entirely your choice.

### Day 3 · Alexandria
**The Mediterranean day**

About three hours north by the desert road to the city Alexander the Great founded in 331 BC. The Catacombs of Kom El Shuqafa, used from about the late 1st to the 4th century AD, where Egyptian tomb layouts wear Greek and Roman dress. Pompey's Pillar, a single shaft of red Aswan granite about 27 metres tall, raised in 297 AD for the emperor Diocletian. A seafood lunch by the harbour. In the afternoon, the Greco-Roman Museum, reopened in 2023, and the Roman Theatre at Kom El Dikka, thirteen tiers of white marble. Then the drive back to Giza.

**The day:** Catacombs of Kom El Shuqafa · Pompey's Pillar · Greco-Roman Museum · Kom El Dikka Roman Theatre
**Overnight:** Marriott Mena House, Giza
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private air-conditioned car, Giza → Alexandria → Giza, about three hours each way
**Pace:** A long day, about six hours in the car
