## Opening

This trip goes south from Cairo by road and stops in Middle Egypt: the painted Middle Kingdom tombs of Beni Hassan, the city of Thoth at Hermopolis and its necropolis at Tuna el-Gebel, and Tell el-Amarna, the capital Akhenaten built for a single god. Then the road carries on to Luxor and Aswan, and a flight from Abu Simbel brings you back to Cairo.

---

## Why this route works

Driving the valley in one direction, north to south, means no doubling back: Minya, then Luxor, then Edfu and Kom Ombo on the road to Aswan, and Abu Simbel at the far end. By the time you reach the Valley of the Kings, you've already stood in the city where Akhenaten tried to replace the old gods. The flight north saves the long road home, and Cairo's museum and the first pyramids close the trip.

**The pyramids, then the road south**

### Day 3 · Middle Egypt
**Beni Hassan, Hermopolis, Tuna el-Gebel and Tell el-Amarna**

**Morning.** Beni Hassan, a clifftop cemetery on the east bank with thirty-nine tombs of Middle Kingdom governors, twelve of them open, painted with wrestlers, hunters and daily life.

**Midday.** Hermopolis, ancient Khmun, city of Thoth, with the columns of a basilica built from temple blocks. Then Tuna el-Gebel, its necropolis, with catacombs of sacred ibises and baboons and the tomb of the priest Petosiris, from around 300 BC, which blends Egyptian and Greek art.

**Afternoon.** Tell el-Amarna, Akhenaten's "Horizon of the Aten", built around 1346 BC and abandoned after his death. The courtiers' cliff tombs show the royal family under the rays of the Aten, each ray ending in a small hand.

**The day:** Beni Hassan · Hermopolis (Ashmunein) · Tuna el-Gebel · Tomb of Petosiris · Tell el-Amarna
**Overnight:** Minya hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private air-conditioned car
**Pace:** A long day, with uneven ground at Beni Hassan

### Day 5 · Luxor
**The West Bank and Karnak**

The Valley of the Kings, more than sixty royal tombs from about 1500 to 1070 BC, among them Tutankhamun's, found essentially intact in 1922. Hatshepsut's three terraces at Deir el-Bahari, built for the woman who ruled as pharaoh for some twenty years, and the Colossi of Memnon, two seated figures of Amenhotep III. In the afternoon, Karnak, enlarged over some 1,500 years, with the Great Hypostyle Hall of 134 columns up to 21 metres high.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon · Karnak · Great Hypostyle Hall
**Overnight:** Luxor hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers
**Pace:** The fullest day

**Optional on this day:**
- The tombs of Tutankhamun, Seti I and Ramses VI: extra tickets

### Day 6 · Luxor → Aswan
**Edfu, Kom Ombo and a felucca**

South along the Nile, stopping twice. Edfu's Temple of Horus, built between 237 and 57 BC, with walls, roof and sanctuary intact. Kom Ombo, the double temple of Sobek and Horus the Elder, every chamber mirrored, with its mummified crocodiles. Aswan in the afternoon, and a felucca past Kitchener's Island as the light drops.

**The day:** Temple of Horus at Edfu · Temple of Kom Ombo · Crocodile Museum · felucca
**Overnight:** Aswan hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private car, Luxor → Aswan · felucca

### Day 7 · Aswan
**Philae, the High Dam and the Unfinished Obelisk**

By motorboat to Philae, the Temple of Isis, a Ptolemaic and Roman sanctuary moved block by block to Agilkia Island between 1972 and 1980. The High Dam, completed in 1970, which ended the annual flood and created Lake Nasser over the old Nubian heartland. The Unfinished Obelisk, about 42 metres long, cracked and abandoned in its granite quarry. The afternoon is free.

**The day:** Temple of Philae · Aswan High Dam · Unfinished Obelisk
**Overnight:** Aswan hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers · motorboat to Philae
**Pace:** Free afternoon

### Day 8 · Aswan → Abu Simbel → Cairo
**The temples of Ramses II, then the flight north**

An early, short flight south to Abu Simbel: Ramses II's two cliff temples of the 13th century BC, the four seated figures of the Great Temple twenty metres high, and the Temple of Nefertari beside it. Between 1964 and 1968 both were cut into blocks and rebuilt 65 metres higher, out of reach of Lake Nasser. Then the flight to Cairo.

**The day:** Abu Simbel · Great Temple of Ramses II · Temple of Nefertari
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Domestic flights Aswan → Abu Simbel → Cairo (not included) · private transfers
**Pace:** A day of airports, worth it

### Day 9 · Cairo
**The Grand Egyptian Museum, the Citadel and Khan El Khalili**

The Grand Egyptian Museum, with the complete Tutankhamun collection, now seen after the Valley where it was found. Then the Citadel of Saladin on the Mokattam ridge, which governed Cairo for seven centuries, and the alabaster Mosque of Muhammad Ali. Khan El Khalili, trading since the 14th century, and in the evening a dinner cruise on the Nile.

**The day:** Grand Egyptian Museum · Citadel of Saladin · Mosque of Muhammad Ali · Khan El Khalili · Nile dinner cruise
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner

### Day 10 · Memphis, Saqqara and Dahshur → departure
**Where the pyramid began, then the airport**

Memphis, first capital of a united Egypt, with the fallen colossus of Ramses II and the alabaster sphinx. Saqqara, where the Step Pyramid of Djoser, built around 2650 BC by Imhotep, began the pyramid tradition, with the Imhotep Museum and one of the nobles' tombs. Dahshur, where Sneferu's Bent Pyramid and Red Pyramid show the corrections that led to Giza. Then the transfer to Cairo airport.

**The day:** Memphis · Saqqara · Step Pyramid of Djoser · Imhotep Museum · a nobles' tomb · Dahshur · Bent Pyramid · Red Pyramid

**A good fit if you:**
- have seen Luxor and Aswan, or want more than the usual route
- are drawn to Akhenaten and Amarna, and to tombs few visitors see
- would rather see Egypt at ground level than from a plane window

**Worth knowing:**
- Days 2, 4 and 6 have long drives, Day 4 the longest.
- Real walking on uneven ground at Beni Hassan and Amarna.
- Minya's hotels are simpler than those in Cairo, Luxor and Aswan.
- Day 10 ends at the airport and suits an evening flight. With an earlier one, Dahshur drops out.
