## Opening

No monument waits at either end of this journey. The Aswan airport transfer is exactly what it says: a private car that meets you at the terminal and delivers you to your hotel or your Nile cruise ship, and on your last day takes you back. Its value is in proportion to how tired you are when you land, and how little you want to bargain for a taxi. Book one direction or both; the price is the same each way.

---

## Why the day runs this way

We follow your flight, so your representative is in the arrivals hall when you come through. The airport lies well outside the city, on the west side of the Nile, and the drive takes 30 to 45 minutes, depending on traffic and where you’re staying. On a tight connection to a Nile cruise, that certainty is the whole point. On departure, we set the pickup time with you around your flight.

## Your day

### Arrival · Airport to your hotel or ship

**If you’re arriving from abroad**
Buy your visa stamp at a bank window before the immigration desks, fix it into a blank page of your passport and queue for the entry stamp. Carry cash; cards aren’t always accepted. On a domestic flight from Cairo, you skip this. With meet and assist, we meet you at the gate and help you through.

**Meeting your representative**
After baggage reclaim, your representative waits in the arrivals hall with a sign bearing your name and walks you to a private air-conditioned car.

**The drive into Aswan**
Past low sand-coloured buildings and the first palms, across the river, until the city opens out and, often, the first feluccas come into view.

### Departure · Hotel or ship to the airport

**Pickup at your hotel or ship**
Your driver collects you at the time we agreed, from your hotel or from your cruise ship at its dock, and loads your bags.

**Drop-off at the terminal**
Your driver takes you to the terminal door. With meet and assist, someone helps you through to check-in.
