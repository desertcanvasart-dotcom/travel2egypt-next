## Opening

Six days, two Egypts. First the one the pharaohs built: Giza, Memphis and Saqqara, where the pyramid began. Then the one that was there before them: the Western Desert, the oasis of Bahariya and its Golden Mummies, and a night camped among the chalk formations of the White Desert, before Cairo again and the Grand Egyptian Museum.

---

## Why this route works

The monuments come first, while you’re fresh, and the heaviest sightseeing day is Day 2. Then the desert: a day for the oasis and its tombs, a day by 4×4 to the White Desert and a night in camp, and the drive back. The Grand Egyptian Museum comes at the end, so the collection follows the places its objects came from. The desert days are long; the first and last days are kept light.

### Day 2 · Giza, Memphis and Saqqara
**Where the pyramid was perfected, and where it was invented**

Giza first: the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC. You go inside the pyramid of Khafre or Menkaure, subject to site rules on the day, then the panorama viewpoint, the Great Sphinx and the Valley Temple. South to Memphis, first capital of a united Egypt, with the fallen colossus of Ramses II and an alabaster sphinx. Then Saqqara, where the Step Pyramid of Djoser, built around 2650 BC, shows the experiment that made Giza possible, with the Imhotep Museum and a nobles’ tomb.

**The day:** Giza pyramids · inside Khafre or Menkaure · panorama viewpoint · Great Sphinx · Valley Temple · Memphis · Saqqara · Step Pyramid of Djoser · Imhotep Museum · a nobles’ tomb
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private air-conditioned transfers
**Pace:** The fullest sightseeing day

**Optional on this day:**
- Inside the Great Pyramid of Khufu: extra ticket, subject to availability and site rules on the day
- Inside the Step Pyramid: extra ticket
- A camel ride at the panorama area: extra cost. We work with handlers we know, whose animals are in good condition, and the ride is entirely your choice.

### Day 3 · Cairo → Bahariya
**The oasis and its Golden Mummies**

About four hours southwest across the desert to Bahariya, green with palms and olives in its depression, with the small town of Bawiti at its heart. One collective ticket opens the oasis sites: a few of the gilded mummies found in 1996 in what is now called the Valley of the Golden Mummies, the painted Late Period tombs of Bannentiu and Djed-Amun-ef-ankh, and the Temple of Alexander the Great. A hot spring at the end of the day, and your hotel.

**The day:** Golden Mummies · Tombs of Bannentiu and Djed-Amun-ef-ankh · Temple of Alexander · hot spring
**Overnight:** Bahariya hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private car, Cairo → Bahariya

---

### Day 4 · The Black Desert → the White Desert
**Crystal Mountain, Agabat and a night under the formations**

By 4×4 south through the Black Desert, a field of dark, basalt-capped hills; Crystal Mountain, a small outcrop of crystals that catches the light; and the Agabat valley, its pale spires best walked rather than photographed from the road. In the afternoon, the White Desert National Park: chalk carved by the wind into Mushroom Rock, towers and columns, long shadows at dusk. Camp among them, with dinner by the fire. The temperature drops sharply after dark, in every season.

**The day:** Black Desert · Crystal Mountain · Agabat · White Desert National Park
**Overnight:** Camping in the White Desert
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private 4×4
**Pace:** The longest day in the desert

---

### Day 5 · The White Desert → Cairo
**The road back, and the Grand Egyptian Museum**

Breakfast at camp, then north by 4×4 to Bahariya and on by car to Cairo. In the afternoon, the Grand Egyptian Museum, at the edge of the Giza plateau, with the complete Tutankhamun collection. After the desert, your Egyptologist keeps the visit to the galleries that matter most to you rather than walking every one. The evening is free.

**The day:** Grand Egyptian Museum · Tutankhamun galleries
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private 4×4 and car, White Desert → Bahariya → Cairo · private transfers
**Pace:** A long drive, then the museum

## Is this journey for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- want the pyramids and a real night in the desert in under a week
- are drawn as much to landscape as to monuments
- would rather camp in the White Desert than spend more days in central Cairo

**Worth knowing:**
- Days 3, 4 and 5 are mostly on the road, and Day 5 is a long drive before the museum.
- The desert camp is simple: tents, a campfire dinner and the stars.
- Desert nights are cold, especially in winter. Bring warm layers.
- The pyramid interior means crouching in a narrow passage.
