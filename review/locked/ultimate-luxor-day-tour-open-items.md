# The Ultimate Luxor Day Tour: open items

Source: the live page `/the-ultimate-luxor-day-tour`, read 17 Nov 2026. Owner answers received 22 Nov 2026.

## Resolved (22 Nov), applied in all three languages

- **Five sites, not six.**
  - **West Bank:** the Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut's temple and the Colossi of Memnon.
  - **East Bank:** Karnak and Luxor Temple.
  - **Removed:** the Valley of the Queens, with every mention of Nefertari's tomb, since the tour doesn't go there.
- **Order:** the West Bank in the morning, then lunch, then the East Bank in the afternoon, ending at Luxor Temple. "Why the day runs this way" explains this: the tombs and Hatshepsut's terraces are best before the heat. The old East-first order is gone.
- **Duration:** about 10 hours. This appears in the panel, in "Why the day runs this way" and in "Worth knowing".
- **Title:** "The Ultimate Luxor Day Tour: Both Banks in One Day", replacing "Both Banks, Six Monuments". The closing line now says "Five sites, two banks". The URL is unchanged, so no redirect is needed.
- **Tutankhamun's tomb:** not included; offered as an optional extra ticket.
- **Not related to the Grand West Bank:** each product stands on its own. The Grand West Bank page still points here for anyone who also wants Karnak and Luxor Temple, which remains correct.
- **No tiers and no age line.** The "Children under 12: 50% off" row is added. The "no minimum age" clause is gone; the honest note for families with young children stays.

## Figures

- About 10 hours
- Valley of the Kings 1550–1070 BC; more than sixty tombs; three per ticket
- Hatshepsut around 1473–1458 BC
- Colossi about 18 m, around 1350 BC
- Karnak about 1,500 years; 134 columns, 21 m
- Over 40°C from May to September
