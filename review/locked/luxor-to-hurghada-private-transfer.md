## Opening

From the Nile Valley to the Red Sea in about four hours: irrigated fields, limestone escarpments, open desert and then, almost without warning, the long blue line of the sea. This private transfer is the comfortable way to make the crossing, with your own car and driver, your own departure time and your luggage taken care of. No stops, no commentary; just the desert going past.

---

## Why the day runs this way

The road is straightforward and well kept, about 290 kilometres across the Eastern Desert. You choose the departure: one last Luxor morning before you go, or an early start to reach the coast with the afternoon still to use. Compared with flying, there’s no airport at either end; compared with the bus, you leave when you want.

## Your day

### Morning · Luxor to the Red Sea

**Pickup from your hotel**
Your driver collects you at the agreed time in a private air-conditioned car.

**Across the Eastern Desert**
The green strip of the valley gives way to the buff and ochre of the desert plateau: low hills, dry wadis, the occasional settlement and the kind of open horizon that is hard to find anywhere. No single dramatic moment, just a gradual change that is quietly absorbing if you watch it.

**The Red Sea Mountains and the coast**
After three hours or so, the jagged coastal range appears, then the descent begins, and the sea comes into view before the city does. Your driver delivers you to your hotel or resort.
