## Opening

Cairo first: the step pyramid at Saqqara, Old Cairo's churches, the Egyptian Museum and the pyramids at Giza. Then a flight south to Luxor and four nights on a cruise up the river to Aswan, with Karnak, the Valley of the Kings, Edfu, Kom Ombo and Philae on the way. You travel with a small group of four to eight people and one licensed Egyptologist.

---

## Why this route works

The oldest sites come first, so Giza on Day 3 makes sense after you have seen how the pyramid began. The cruise starts in Luxor, so the busiest sightseeing falls early while you're fresh, and the last days in Aswan are slower. A flight at each end of the river saves two long drives.

## Your nine days

### Day 1 · Cairo
**Arrival**

You're met at Cairo airport, helped through arrival formalities and taken to your hotel. In the evening, a welcome dinner with your group at a local restaurant.

**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Dinner
**Travel:** Meet and assist at the airport, transfer to the hotel

---

### Day 2 · Memphis, Saqqara and Old Cairo
**Where the pyramid began, and the city's Christian quarter**

Memphis, first capital of a united Egypt around 3100 BC, with the fallen colossus of Ramses II. Saqqara and the Step Pyramid of Djoser, built around 2650 BC, the first pyramid and the experiment that made Giza possible. After lunch at a local restaurant, Old Cairo: the Hanging Church, built over a gate of the Roman fortress of Babylon, and the lanes of the Coptic quarter.

**The day:** Memphis · Saqqara · Step Pyramid of Djoser · Hanging Church · Coptic Cairo
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Transfers with your group

---

### Day 3 · The Egyptian Museum and Giza
**The pharaohs' collection, then the pyramids**

The Egyptian Museum on Tahrir Square, opened in 1902, with its collection from every period of ancient Egypt. Then the Giza plateau: the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC, the Great Sphinx and the Valley Temple below it. Dinner with your group at a local restaurant.

**The day:** Egyptian Museum · Giza pyramids · Great Sphinx · Valley Temple
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · dinner
**Travel:** Transfers with your group

**Optional on this day:**
- Inside one of the pyramids: extra ticket

---

### Day 4 · Cairo → Luxor · the East Bank
**Karnak and Luxor Temple, and on board**

An early flight to Luxor. Karnak, a temple city added to over fifteen centuries, with the Great Hypostyle Hall of 134 columns up to 21 metres, then Luxor Temple by the river, at the end of the Avenue of Sphinxes. On board your cruise for lunch.

**The day:** Karnak · Great Hypostyle Hall · Luxor Temple · Avenue of Sphinxes
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Luxor
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Domestic flight Cairo → Luxor (not included) · transfers

---

### Day 5 · Luxor · the West Bank
**The Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut and the Colossi**

The Valley of the Kings, more than sixty royal tombs cut into the cliffs, their corridors painted with scenes from the Book of the Dead. Hatshepsut's colonnaded temple at Deir el-Bahari, and the Colossi of Memnon, two seated figures of Amenhotep III about 18 metres tall. Back on board, the cruise starts south.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Transfers · by cruise

**Optional on this day:**
- A hot-air balloon over the West Bank at sunrise: extra
- The tombs of Tutankhamun, Seti I and Ramses VI: extra tickets

---

### Day 6 · Edfu and Kom Ombo
**Two river temples**

Edfu's Temple of Horus, its enclosure wall, roof, halls and sanctuary standing as completed in the 1st century BC. In the afternoon, Kom Ombo on a bend of the river, its plan doubled for Sobek and Horus the Elder, with mummified crocodiles in the museum. The cruise sails on to Aswan.

**The day:** Temple of Horus at Edfu · Temple of Kom Ombo · Crocodile Museum
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** By cruise

---

### Day 7 · Aswan
**A felucca, and a free afternoon**

A felucca, the Nile's sailing boat, past Elephantine Island and the Aga Khan Mausoleum. The rest of the day is yours, for the Aswan souq or the ship's deck.

**The day:** Felucca · Elephantine Island · Aga Khan Mausoleum (from the water) · free time
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Aswan
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Felucca
**Pace:** An easy day

---

### Day 8 · Aswan → Cairo
**Philae, the High Dam, and Khan El Khalili**

Off the cruise after breakfast. By motorboat to Philae, the Temple of Isis, moved to Agilkia Island above the new waterline. The Aswan High Dam, completed in 1970, which made Lake Nasser. Then the flight to Cairo, and an evening in Khan El Khalili, the bazaar founded in the 14th century, with dinner at a local restaurant.

**The day:** Temple of Philae · Aswan High Dam · Khan El Khalili
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · dinner
**Travel:** Motorboat · domestic flight Aswan → Cairo (not included) · transfers

---

### Day 9 · Cairo
**Departure**

Breakfast and the transfer to Cairo airport.

**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Transfer to the airport
