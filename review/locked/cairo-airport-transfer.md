## Opening

There’s no glamorous way to land, tired, in a city of twenty million, and no relaxed way to leave it if you’re watching the clock. This private transfer handles both ends: a representative meets you when you arrive and drives you to your hotel, and on your last day collects you from the lobby and takes you to your terminal. Book one direction or both; the price is the same each way.

---

## Why the day runs this way

We follow your flight, so on arrival the car waits on you, not the other way round. The drive is the one honest variable: from the airport to central Cairo or Giza it takes anywhere from 35 minutes to well over an hour, and late-night arrivals tend to move faster than afternoon ones. On departure, Cairo’s traffic decides everything, so we set your pickup time the evening before, around your flight and the likely traffic, to give you a real buffer, not a theoretical one. If your flight moves, the pickup moves with it.

## Your day

### Arrival · Airport to your hotel

**Visa and immigration**
If you need a visa on arrival, buy it at the bank counters in the arrivals terminal, before the immigration desks. Fix the visa stamp into a blank page of your passport, then queue for the entry stamp and collect your luggage. With meet and assist, we meet you at the gate and help you through all of this.

**Meeting your representative**
In the public arrivals hall beyond the exit, your representative waits with a sign bearing your name and walks you to the car.

**The drive to your hotel**
A private air-conditioned car, not shared. The route skirts the eastern and northern edges of the city, and depending on your hotel, you may catch a first glimpse of the Nile or the skyline towards Giza.

### Departure · Hotel to the airport

**Pickup in your hotel lobby**
Your representative waits in the lobby at the time we agreed and walks you to a private air-conditioned car.

**The drive**
Depending on your hotel, you may pass the older streets of Heliopolis, the wide avenues of eastern Cairo or the elevated expressways, with the skyline falling behind you.

**Drop-off at your terminal**
Your driver takes you to the terminal for your airline, not just to the airport. With meet and assist, someone helps you through to check-in.
