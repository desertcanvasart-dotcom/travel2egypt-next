## Opening

Two days in Cairo for the Grand Egyptian Museum, the pyramids and the old city. Then a flight to Aswan, Abu Simbel by road, three nights on a cruise down the river to Luxor, two nights by the Red Sea at Hurghada, and a last afternoon at the Citadel above Cairo. You travel with a small group of four to eight people and one licensed Egyptologist.

---

## Why this route works

The first day asks nothing, because flights from Europe often land late. The cruise sails with the current from Aswan to Luxor, and when the ship reaches Luxor in time for the East Bank, the last morning there is only the West Bank. From Luxor, Hurghada is a drive, and the free day at the coast comes before the flight back to Cairo.

## Your ten days

### Day 1 · Cairo
**Arrival**

You're met at Cairo airport, helped through arrival formalities and taken to your hotel. Nothing else is planned.

**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Travel:** Meet and assist at the airport, transfer to the hotel

---

### Day 2 · The Grand Egyptian Museum and Giza
**Tutankhamun's treasures, then the pyramids**

The Grand Egyptian Museum beside the Giza plateau, with the complete Tutankhamun collection shown together. Then the plateau: the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC, and the Great Sphinx below them. Lunch at a local restaurant.

**The day:** Grand Egyptian Museum · Giza pyramids · Great Sphinx
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Transfers with your group

**Optional on this day:**
- Inside one of the pyramids: extra ticket

---

### Day 3 · Old Cairo, Islamic Cairo and Khan El Khalili
**The city's churches, mosques and bazaar**

Old Cairo: the Hanging Church, built over a gate of the Roman fortress of Babylon, and the lanes of the Coptic quarter. Then Islamic Cairo's medieval streets and mosques, and Khan El Khalili, the bazaar founded in the 14th century. Lunch at a local restaurant.

**The day:** Hanging Church · Coptic Cairo · Islamic Cairo · Khan El Khalili
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Transfers with your group

---

### Day 4 · Cairo → Aswan
**The High Dam, Philae, and on board**

A morning flight to Aswan. The Aswan High Dam, completed in 1970, which made Lake Nasser. By motorboat to Philae, the Temple of Isis, moved to Agilkia Island above the new waterline. Then on board your cruise, moored in Aswan for the night.

**The day:** Aswan High Dam · Temple of Philae
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Aswan
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Domestic flight Cairo → Aswan (not included) · transfers · motorboat

---

### Day 5 · Abu Simbel → Kom Ombo
**The temples of Ramses II, then a double temple on the river**

An early start, about three and a half hours south by road to Abu Simbel: Ramses II's two cliff temples of the 13th century BC, their four seated figures twenty metres high. Between 1964 and 1968 they were cut into blocks and rebuilt 65 metres higher, out of reach of Lake Nasser. Back on board in Aswan for lunch, and the cruise sails north to Kom Ombo, its plan doubled for Sobek and Horus the Elder, with mummified crocodiles in the museum. Then on toward Edfu.

**The day:** Abu Simbel · Great Temple of Ramses II · Temple of Nefertari · Temple of Kom Ombo · Crocodile Museum
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Road Aswan ⇄ Abu Simbel · by cruise
**Pace:** A long day, with a very early start

---

### Day 6 · Edfu → Luxor · the East Bank
**The Temple of Horus, the Esna lock, and Karnak**

Edfu's Temple of Horus, its enclosure wall, roof, halls and sanctuary standing as completed in the 1st century BC. Through the lock at Esna to Luxor. In the late afternoon, if the ship is in on time, Karnak, a temple city added to over fifteen centuries, with the Great Hypostyle Hall of 134 columns up to 21 metres, and Luxor Temple by the river as the lights come on. If the ship arrives late, both temples move to the next morning.

**The day:** Temple of Horus at Edfu · Esna lock · Karnak · Luxor Temple
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Luxor
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** By cruise · transfers

---

### Day 7 · Luxor → Hurghada
**The West Bank, then east to the Red Sea**

Off the cruise after breakfast. The Valley of the Kings, more than sixty royal tombs cut into the cliffs. Hatshepsut's colonnaded temple at Deir el-Bahari, and the Colossi of Memnon, two seated figures of Amenhotep III about 18 metres tall. Then about four hours across the Eastern Desert to Hurghada, where your all-inclusive hotel starts with lunch.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon
**Overnight:** Hurghada hotel, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Transfers · road Luxor → Hurghada

**Optional on this day:**
- A hot-air balloon over the West Bank at sunrise: extra
- The tombs of Tutankhamun, Seti I and Ramses VI: extra tickets

---

### Day 8 · Hurghada
**A free day by the Red Sea**

The day is yours: the beach, the pool, or a boat to the islands and the reefs.

**Overnight:** Hurghada hotel, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** Free day

**Optional on this day:**
- Snorkelling, diving or a boat trip to the islands: extra

---

### Day 9 · Hurghada → Cairo
**The Citadel and the Mosque of Muhammad Ali**

The flight back to Cairo. The Citadel on the Mokattam ridge, which commanded Cairo for seven centuries, and the alabaster Mosque of Muhammad Ali, with the whole city below. Dinner at the hotel.

**The day:** Citadel of Saladin · Mosque of Muhammad Ali
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Domestic flight Hurghada → Cairo (not included) · transfers

---

### Day 10 · Cairo
**Departure**

Breakfast and the transfer to Cairo airport.

**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Transfer to the airport
