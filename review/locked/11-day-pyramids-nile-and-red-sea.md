## Opening

The three halves of a first visit to Egypt in eleven days, at a family pace: the Giza pyramids, a four-night Nile cruise from Aswan to Luxor, three days on the Red Sea at Hurghada, and Cairo again to close with the Grand Egyptian Museum and the oldest pyramids.

---

## Why this route works

Egypt rewards a trip that moves in one direction and keeps one real rest in the middle. The pyramids come first, as the headline. The river comes next, sailing north with the current of the story, from Aswan, where the Nile narrows, to Luxor, where Egypt’s empire sat. Then the sea, once five days of early starts have earned it. Cairo comes last, when the museum can show you what you have just seen in the ground.

## Your eleven days

### Day 1 · Cairo
**Arrival and Giza**

You’re met at Cairo airport and taken straight to the Giza plateau: the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC, and the Great Sphinx below them. If you land late in the day, we move Giza to the afternoon of Day 9, beside the museum.

**The day:** Giza pyramids · Great Sphinx
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Lunch
**Travel:** Meet and greet at the airport · private transfers
**Pace:** Easy

**Optional on this day:**
- Inside one of the pyramids: extra ticket

---

### Day 2 · Cairo → Aswan
**The High Dam, the Unfinished Obelisk and a felucca**

A morning flight south to Aswan, where you board your Nile cruise ship. In the afternoon, the High Dam, completed in 1970, which created Lake Nasser and drowned much of ancient Nubia. The Unfinished Obelisk, 42 metres long, abandoned in its granite quarry with a crack through it, shows exactly how the ancient stonecutters worked. At the end of the day, a felucca around Kitchener’s Island, planted as a garden in the 1890s.

**The day:** Aswan High Dam · Unfinished Obelisk · felucca
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Aswan
**Meals:** Lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Domestic flight Cairo → Aswan · private transfers · felucca

---

### Day 3 · Aswan → Kom Ombo
**Philae, and sailing north**

In the morning, by motorboat to Philae, the temple of Isis, rebuilt block by block on Agilkia Island when Lake Nasser rose, one of the last temples built in the old style. The afternoon is yours, on board or ashore. In the evening the ship sails north to Kom Ombo.

**The day:** Temple of Philae
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Kom Ombo
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfers · motorboat to Philae · by cruise
**Pace:** Easy afternoon

**Optional on this day:**
- Abu Simbel by road, a long day south to the temples of Ramesses II, leaving before dawn and back on board by midday, with Philae in the afternoon: extra

---

### Day 4 · Kom Ombo → Edfu → Esna
**The double temple and the temple of Horus**

Kom Ombo, with two of every doorway and sanctuary for Sobek and Horus the Elder, and its mummified crocodiles. The ship sails on to Edfu, where you go ashore by horse-drawn carriage to the Temple of Horus, built between 237 and 57 BC: not the oldest temple in Egypt, but the most complete, with its roof, walls, sanctuary and pylon intact. The ship then sails north through the evening.

**The day:** Temple of Kom Ombo · Temple of Horus at Edfu
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Esna
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** By cruise · horse-drawn carriage

---

### Day 5 · Luxor
**The West Bank and Karnak**

The ship reaches Luxor in the morning. The Valley of the Kings, with more than sixty painted royal tombs, among them Tutankhamun’s. The three terraces of Hatshepsut’s temple at Deir el-Bahari, built for the pharaoh her successors tried to erase, and the Colossi of Memnon, about 720 tons each. In the afternoon, Karnak, enlarged over some 1,500 years, with a Great Hypostyle Hall of 134 sandstone columns up to 21 metres tall.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon · Karnak
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Luxor
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfers · by cruise
**Pace:** The fullest day

**Optional on this day:**
- The tombs of Tutankhamun, Seti I and Ramesses VI: extra tickets

---

### Day 6 · Luxor → Hurghada
**Luxor Temple, then east across the desert**

In the morning, Luxor Temple, joined to Karnak by the Avenue of Sphinxes, reopened in November 2021. Then you leave the ship and cross the Eastern Desert to Hurghada, about three hours, and check in to your all-inclusive resort. The rest of the day is free.

**The day:** Luxor Temple · Avenue of Sphinxes
**Overnight:** Red Sea resort, Hurghada, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast (on the ship) · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private car, Luxor → Hurghada
**Pace:** Easy afternoon

---

### Day 7 · Hurghada
**The reef, or nothing at all**

Snorkelling over coral gardens at the Giftun Islands, a protected marine archipelago an hour offshore; for certified divers, diving; a glass-bottom boat, windsurfing or sailing; or the pool and the beach. Nothing is compulsory.

**The day:** your choice
**Overnight:** Red Sea resort, Hurghada, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** As much or as little as you like

---

### Day 8 · Hurghada
**Your day by the Red Sea**

A day left open on purpose. Choose from Day 7’s options, or wander Hurghada’s old town, El Dahar, with its Friday market and small mosques, or do very little.

**Overnight:** Red Sea resort, Hurghada, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** As much or as little as you like

---

### Day 9 · Hurghada → Cairo
**The Grand Egyptian Museum, the Citadel and Khan el-Khalili**

A morning flight to Cairo, and straight to the Grand Egyptian Museum, with the complete Tutankhamun collection and Khufu’s cedar boat. Then the Citadel, on the Mokattam ridge, which dominated Cairo for seven centuries, with the alabaster mosque of Muhammad Ali. Khan el-Khalili, trading since the 14th century, to finish.

**The day:** Grand Egyptian Museum · Citadel of Saladin · Mosque of Muhammad Ali · Khan el-Khalili
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast (at the resort) · lunch
**Travel:** Domestic flight Hurghada → Cairo · private transfers

---

### Day 10 · Cairo
**Memphis, Saqqara and Dahshur, and a Nile dinner cruise**

Memphis, the first capital of a united Egypt, around 3100 BC, with the fallen colossus of Ramesses II and an alabaster sphinx. Saqqara, and the Step Pyramid of Djoser, built by Imhotep around 2650 BC. Dahshur, where the builders learned how to make a true pyramid, at the Bent Pyramid and the Red Pyramid, 105 metres high. In the evening, a dinner cruise on the Nile.

**The day:** Memphis · Saqqara · Step Pyramid · Dahshur · Nile dinner cruise
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfers

---

### Day 11 · Cairo
**Departure**

Breakfast, and the transfer to Cairo airport.

**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Private transfer to the airport
