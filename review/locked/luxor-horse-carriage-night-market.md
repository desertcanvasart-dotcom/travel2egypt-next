## Opening

By day, Luxor is a city of monuments. In the evening, when the heat drops and the streets fill with families, vendors and the clatter of hooves, it becomes a working Egyptian town again. This private carriage ride is simply that: two hours of wandering the evening city, past temples lit up after dark, along the Nile, and then into the local market without ever leaving your seat. No tickets, no queues: just Luxor after dark.

---

## Why the evening runs this way

The evening is when the city is most itself, and a carriage sets the right pace for it: slow enough to look, fast enough to cover the city. The ride starts with the lit temples and the river, while the air is cool and the Corniche is at its liveliest, and ends in the noise and colour of the market. It sits best after a long temple day, when you want Luxor without a lecture.

## Your evening

### Evening · Luxor after dark

**Pickup from your hotel**
Your carriage and English-speaking assistant collect you from your hotel. The driver knows the streets and the pace of a good evening ride.

**The city and the lit temples**
Along the main boulevards and through the older neighbourhoods, past the temples lit up after dark: the sound of the wheels, the warm air, the minarets and the street life on either side. There's no narration and no fixed script; your assistant is there to help, not to lecture.

**Along the Nile**
A stretch beside the river, where Luxor comes out in the evening to walk, talk and watch the boats. The light on the water does the rest.

**Through the local market**
The carriage takes you into Luxor's market of spices, cloth, produce and cheerful miscellany, so you see it from the carriage at an easy pace. If you'd like to stop, browse or try a shisha at one of the market cafés, you can, at your own cost.

After about two hours.
