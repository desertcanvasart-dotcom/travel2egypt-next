## Opening

Four days on Lake Nasser with a boat and crew of your own: Nile perch in the early mornings, kayaks in the inlets, and one day ashore by 4×4 in the desert along the lake’s eastern shore. The lake is the reservoir behind the Aswan High Dam, completed in 1970, and under it lies the Nubian valley whose villages were emptied as the water rose. On most days you see few other boats.

---

## Why this route works

Three nights aboard give you two full days on the water as well as a day off it. The fishing takes the cool hours, early and late; the middle of the day is for shade, lunch and the small inlets the boat itself can’t enter. The desert day sits in the middle, so you go ashore rested and come back to one more evening on the lake.

## Your four days

### Day 1 · Aswan → Lake Nasser
**Board the boat, and the first water**

You’re collected from your hotel in Aswan and driven the short way south past the High Dam to the lake, where the boat and crew are waiting. Lunch aboard, then the afternoon goes to learning the water: the usual method is trolling lures along the drop-offs, where the old valley floor falls away under the surface. Your guides carry the depth charts. Dinner on board as the desert behind the eastern shore goes dark.

**The day:** boarding on the lake · trolling for Nile perch · sunset at anchor
**Overnight:** private fishing boat on Lake Nasser
**Meals:** Lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Pickup from your Aswan hotel · private car to the lake · private boat
**Pace:** An easy start, then the afternoon on the water

---

### Day 2 · On Lake Nasser
**Fish, a quiet cove, and the kayaks**

The morning bite is early, before the wind picks up, and that is when the serious fishing happens. Your guides choose the coves and the depth by the lake level and the season. Tigerfish, smaller and quicker, take lures in the shallower water, and there are catfish and tilapia along the margins. Around midday, lunch in a sheltered cove and a rest in the shade. In the afternoon, fish again, or take a kayak into the inlets where the boat can’t follow. After dinner, fish from the deck at night, then sleep in your cabin or on deck under the stars.

**The day:** dawn fishing · tigerfish in the shallows · lunch in a sheltered cove · kayaking in the inlets · night fishing from the deck
**Overnight:** private fishing boat on Lake Nasser
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private boat · kayak
**Pace:** An early start, a quiet middle of the day

---

### Day 3 · The desert shore
**A day ashore by 4×4**

The boat puts you ashore on the eastern side of the lake, and a 4×4 takes you into the desert behind it: dunes, outcrops of dark basalt and pale limestone, and, with luck, the tracks of gazelles and jackals in the sand. Your guide shows you where the valley was lived in before the 1960s, where the river once ran and which villages now lie offshore. Back aboard for an evening session on the water.

**The day:** 4×4 in the desert · dunes and rock outcrops · wildlife tracks · evening fishing
**Overnight:** private fishing boat on Lake Nasser
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private boat · private 4×4
**Pace:** A day on land, then the lake again

---

### Day 4 · Lake Nasser → Aswan
**A last morning on the lake**

A final session at first light, then breakfast and the run back towards the High Dam. On the way your guides point out where Nubian villages stood before the water rose: about 100,000 Nubians were resettled, many of them near Kom Ombo. Lunch aboard, then you leave the boat and are driven back to your hotel in Aswan.

**The day:** dawn fishing · the return across the lake · the drive to Aswan
**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private boat · private car to your Aswan hotel
**Pace:** An early outing, then the return

## Is this journey for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- fish, and want more than fishing from the trip
- want a part of Egypt with no monument schedule
- like open landscape and very dark night skies
- are a small party and want the boat to yourselves

**Worth knowing:**
- Catches and wildlife sightings can’t be guaranteed. The fish move with the lake level, the water temperature and the season.
- The boat is simple and practical, not a floating hotel.
- Early starts and long hours in the sun. Bring sun protection, a hat and a warm layer for the cool mornings and nights.
- The temples on the lake are not part of the standard programme.
