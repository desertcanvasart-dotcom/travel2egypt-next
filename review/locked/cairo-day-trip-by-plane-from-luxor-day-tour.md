## Opening

Luxor has the temples and the tombs; Cairo has a different scale altogether: louder, denser, with monuments photographs still can’t prepare you for. This private day flies you north for the pyramids of Giza, Tutankhamun’s complete treasure at the Grand Egyptian Museum and the lanes of Khan El Khalili, and flies you back to your Luxor hotel by night. One day isn’t enough to know Cairo, but it’s enough to see what the fuss is about.

---

## Why the day runs this way

The flight covers about 670 kilometres in under an hour, so with an early flight you land in Cairo by mid-morning. The plateau comes first, then the museum next door, then the bazaar before the airport. Fourteen hours sounds generous, but the flights take their share and the Cairo window is finite: each of the three stops would happily fill it alone. Tell us which matters most, and we’ll weight the day around it.

## Your day

### Morning · The flight and the Giza Plateau

**Pickup and the flight north**
Your driver takes you to Luxor airport. In Cairo, your Egyptologist meets you on arrival.

**The pyramids of Giza**
Khufu’s, Khafre’s and Menkaure’s, built across three generations from around 2560 BC, spread over a larger plateau than photographs suggest. What strikes you first isn’t the height but the volume: Khufu’s holds an estimated 2.3 million blocks.
*Sites:* Great Pyramid of Khufu · Pyramid of Khafre · Pyramid of Menkaure

**Inside a pyramid (included).** You go inside the pyramid of Khafre or Menkaure, subject to site rules and availability on the day.

**The Great Sphinx**
Cut from the bedrock, 73 metres long, restored many times, its watchful expression still intact.
*Sites:* Great Sphinx

**Optional extras, if you’ve chosen them**
- Inside the Great Pyramid of Khufu: extra ticket, subject to availability and site rules on the day
- A camel ride: extra cost. We work with handlers we know, whose animals are in good condition, and the ride is entirely your choice.

### Midday · Lunch

**Lunch at a local restaurant (included)**
Drinks aren’t included.

### Afternoon · The Grand Egyptian Museum and the bazaar

**The Grand Egyptian Museum**
Fully open since 1 November 2025, at the plateau’s edge. Here is the complete Tutankhamun collection, more than 5,000 objects from his tomb, including the gold mask and the nested coffins, plus the grand staircase of royal statues and galleries from prehistory to the Romans. A museum that rewards slow attention; your guide focuses the visit.
*Sites:* Grand Egyptian Museum · Tutankhamun galleries

**Khan El Khalili**
Busy, loud and trading since the 14th century: spices, gold, papyrus, inlaid wood and blown glass. Stop for tea on Al-Hussein Square before the airport.
*Sites:* Khan El Khalili

**The flight back to Luxor**
A driver meets you at Luxor airport and takes you to your hotel.
