# Minya Day Tour: open items

Source: the live page `/minya-day-tour`, read 17 Nov 2026. Owner answers received 22 Nov 2026.

## Resolved (22 Nov), applied in all three languages

- **Order:** the east bank first (Beni Hassan, then Amarna), lunch, then across to the west bank (Hermopolis and Tuna el-Gebel). "Why the day runs this way" now explains this: one crossing each way, with no doubling back. The morning and afternoon headings follow the same order.
- **Tuna el-Gebel added** to this day tour. It gets its own stop, an entry in "Included" and a place in the title. The description is the approved wording from the 2-day Minya tour: the necropolis, the catacombs of ibises and baboons, and the tomb of Petosiris, around 300 BC.
- **Title:** "Minya Day Tour from Cairo: Beni Hassan, Amarna, Hermopolis & Tuna el-Gebel". The URL `/minya-day-tour` is unchanged, so no redirect is needed.
- **The 2-day tour exists:** "Make it your own" now points to it by name ("our two-day Minya and Amarna tour") and describes it as the 2-day page does.
- **Security:** a calm line in "Worth knowing": routine checkpoints along the way, there for travellers' safety, nothing to worry about.
- **Entrance tickets:** all included (Beni Hassan, Amarna, Hermopolis, Tuna el-Gebel).

## Figures

- About 250 km south; about 3 hours each way; about 12 hours in all
- Beni Hassan: 39 tombs, about 2000 BC
- Amarna founded around 1346 BC; about 17 years
- Hermopolis: Greek period after 332 BC
- Petosiris around 300 BC

## Numeric gate (22 Nov)

- **EN and ES:** 6, 12, 50, 250, 300, 332, 1346, 2000. Spoken numbers such as "thirty-nine" and "three hours" are written as words.
- **JA:** the same figures, plus extra digits from Japanese writing conventions, none of them a new fact:
  - 1 (1名様, 1日, 1泊)
  - 2 (2日間, "two-day")
  - 3 (アメンヘテプ3世 "Amenhotep III")
  - 10 (10月6日市)
  - 17 (約17年, "about seventeen years")
  - 39 (39基, "thirty-nine tombs")
