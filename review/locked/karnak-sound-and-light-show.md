## Opening

By day, Karnak rewards slow exploration. After dark, it rewards something else: sitting still and letting the scale land. The Sound and Light Show uses the temple itself as its stage, with light on the pylons and columns and a narration that runs from Ramses II’s campaigns to the power of Amun’s priests. It’s the gentlest evening Luxor offers: seated, unhurried, an hour among the lit columns.

---

## Why the day runs this way

The show keeps its own schedule, which changes with the seasons, so the evening is set around it. Most Luxor hotels are within twenty minutes of Karnak, on the northern edge of town. It works best after your daytime temple visit, when the hall you walked at noon comes back in another key, or as a soft first night before it.

## Your day

### Evening · Karnak

**Pickup from your hotel**
Your driver collects you in a private air-conditioned car.

**The walk through the temple**
The show leads you in along the processional avenue, through the unfinished first pylon, begun in the 4th century BC, and into the Great Hypostyle Hall of Seti I and Ramses II: 134 columns, the tallest 21 metres high. With light moving over the reliefs, the hall’s density becomes almost overwhelming, in the best way.
*Sites:* Karnak · Great Hypostyle Hall

**The sacred lake**
The narration follows the dynasties that built Karnak, Akhenaten’s break with Amun and the restoration that followed. The show ends at the sacred lake, dug under Thutmose III, its still water reflecting the lit obelisks.
*Sites:* Sacred Lake
