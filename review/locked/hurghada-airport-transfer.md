## Opening

An airport transfer isn’t a tour. It’s the handover between the aircraft door and your hotel lobby, and back again at the end of your stay, and it should be described as exactly that. A representative meets you when you land and a private air-conditioned car takes you to your hotel, in Hurghada itself or along the coast; on your last day, the same car takes you back. Book one direction or both; the price is the same each way.

---

## Why the day runs this way

The honest variable is geography. Central Hurghada is minutes from the airport, while El Gouna to the north, or Soma Bay and Safaga to the south, can be an hour or more by road, and El Quseir nearer two. Give us your hotel and your flight number, and we time the car to the real distance. The longer part of an arrival is usually the airport, not the drive: when several charter flights land together, the visa and immigration queues can take twenty to forty minutes.

## Your day

### Arrival · Airport to your hotel

**Visa and immigration**
If you need a visa on arrival, buy it at the bank counters inside the terminal, before the immigration desks, not after. Fix the stamp into a blank page of your passport and join the queue for the arrival seal. With meet and assist, we meet you at the gate and help you through.

**Baggage**
Check the carousel screen for your flight number, collect everything and move through customs into the arrivals hall.

**Meeting your representative**
Your representative waits in the meet-and-greet area beyond the exit gate, with a sign bearing the Travel2Egypt name and your own. If you can’t see them straight away, stay in the arrivals hall and wait by the exit.

**The drive to your hotel**
A private air-conditioned car, not shared. The road runs close to the coast for much of the way.

### Departure · Hotel to the airport

**Pickup at your hotel**
Your driver collects you at the time we agreed and loads your bags.

**Drop-off at the terminal**
Your driver takes you to the terminal door. With meet and assist, someone helps you through to check-in.

## Is this day for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- are arriving in Egypt for the first time and don’t know the visa sequence yet
- are travelling with family or a lot of luggage
- are landing late in the evening

**Worth knowing:**
- The visa fee is a government charge, paid in cash at the airport bank counter, not through us. Check the current fee and your nationality’s rules before you fly; some nationalities need an e-visa in advance.
- There’s no guide on this transfer: it’s point to point.
