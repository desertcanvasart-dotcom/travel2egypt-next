## Opening

The Giza Plateau is one morning’s work if it’s done in the right order. This shared half day takes a small group of four to eight travellers, with one licensed Egyptologist, through the three pyramids, out to the desert viewpoint where they line up in one frame, and down to the Sphinx and its Valley Temple. You’re back at your hotel around midday, with the afternoon your own.

---

## Why the day runs this way

The 08:00 start puts you on the plateau before the late-morning heat. The pyramids come first, while the light is still soft, then the panorama, then the Sphinx, which sits lowest and closest to the town. There’s no lunch and no second site: the morning is given to Giza alone, and it ends while there’s still a day left to use.

## Your day

### Morning · The Giza Plateau

**08:00 · Pickup from your hotel**
Your driver and Egyptologist collect you and the rest of the group in an air-conditioned vehicle, with a short briefing on the way.

**The three pyramids**
Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure were built within about eighty years of each other, around 2500 BC. The Great Pyramid of Khufu is the last of the Seven Wonders of the ancient world still standing: at 146 metres it was the tallest man-made structure on earth for nearly four thousand years, and it is aligned to true north within a fraction of a degree. Khafre’s keeps a band of its original polished casing near the top; Menkaure’s is the smallest of the three.
*Sites:* Great Pyramid of Khufu · Pyramid of Khafre · Pyramid of Menkaure

**The panorama viewpoint**
A rise in the desert where all three pyramids line up in one frame, the angle that shows how they relate to each other. Your guide sets the plateau in its wider royal landscape. This is the photography stop.

**Optional extras, if you’ve chosen them**
- Going inside one of the pyramids: extra ticket, subject to availability and site rules on the day
- A short camel ride near the panorama: extra cost. We work with handlers we know, whose animals are in good condition, and the ride is entirely your choice.

**The Great Sphinx and the Valley Temple**
The Sphinx, 73 metres long and 20 metres high, carved from a single limestone outcrop during Khafre’s reign: the largest monolithic statue in the world. Beside it, the Valley Temple of Khafre, built of massive granite blocks.
*Sites:* Great Sphinx · Valley Temple of Khafre

## Is this day for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- want Giza seen properly, with a guide, in a morning rather than a full day
