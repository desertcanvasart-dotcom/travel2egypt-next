## Opening

From the ground, Cairo is a string of separate sites. From the air you see how they fit together: the three pyramids on their plateau, the Nile running through the middle of the city, the Citadel on its spur of the Muqattam Hills. This is a private helicopter flight of 40 to 60 minutes for up to three passengers. Most of the three hours is the transfer, check-in and the safety briefing, and we’d rather you knew that now.

---

## Why the day runs this way

The light decides the slot. Early in the morning, the low sun throws the pyramid faces into relief; in the last hour before sunset, the Nile turns gold. At midday the plateau is bright and flat. So we book early-morning or late-afternoon flights first, and time your pickup to them. The flight leaves from Almaza Airport in Heliopolis, in the north-east of the city. The route below is the planned one; on the day, the pilot and air-traffic control have the last word.

### In the air · Giza, the Nile and the Citadel

**The Giza Plateau**
The three pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC, and their alignment, which you only really read from above. The Great Sphinx faces east at the plateau’s edge, and from the air its place in Khafre’s complex is clear.
*Sites:* Giza Plateau · Great Sphinx

**The Nile through the city**
The river is Cairo’s spine; everything orients to it. Gezira Island sits mid-river, with the Cairo Tower, 187 metres of concrete lattice from 1961, at its northern end.
*Sites:* The Nile · Gezira Island · Cairo Tower

**The Citadel and Islamic Cairo**
Saladin’s Citadel, begun in the 1170s on a spur of the Muqattam Hills because the height made it defensible, and below it the medieval city of al-Qahira, founded in 969, its minarets close to countable from above.
*Sites:* Cairo Citadel · Islamic Cairo

### Afterwards · Back to your hotel

**Landing at Almaza**
The flight returns over the modern skyline and lands at Almaza, where our driver takes you back to your hotel.

## Is this day for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- have seen the pyramids from the ground and want to understand how Cairo fits together
- are a couple or a party of up to three

**Worth knowing:**
- It’s 40 to 60 minutes in the air: an overview, not an education.
- Flights depend on the weather and on permission on the day. In spring, the khamsin sandstorms can ground flights at short notice; we confirm the rescheduling terms in writing when you book.
- The operator sets the passenger rules, such as identification and weight limits. We send them with your booking confirmation.
- Photographs through the cabin window pick up glare in bright light. Treat them as a bonus, not the point.
