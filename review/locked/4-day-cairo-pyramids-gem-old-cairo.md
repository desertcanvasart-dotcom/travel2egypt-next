## Opening

Most flights from the Gulf land in Cairo around midday, which usually means a lost first afternoon. This tour uses it. Lunch and the Grand Egyptian Museum on the day you land, the pyramids of Giza and the old city the next, and the pyramid fields of Memphis, Saqqara and Dahshur on the third, with an early transfer home on the fourth.

---

## Why this route works

The museum comes first, so the first afternoon gives you the overview: the objects, the faces and the names you’ll meet in stone over the next two days. Day 2 takes Giza in the morning, then crosses the city to the Coptic quarter, the Citadel and the bazaar. Day 3 goes back further, to the pyramid fields south of Cairo, where Djoser’s Step Pyramid at Saqqara and Sneferu’s pyramids at Dahshur show how the Egyptians got from steps to the smooth sides of Giza. Day 4 is kept free for an early flight.

## Your four days

### Day 1 · Arrival → the Grand Egyptian Museum
**Lunch, the museum, then your hotel**

**Midday.** You’re met at Cairo airport as you land, and go first to lunch at a local restaurant.

**Afternoon.** The Grand Egyptian Museum, at the edge of the Giza plateau. It brings together objects once scattered across several institutions, many of them in storage for decades: Tutankhamun’s treasures shown in full, statuary, papyri, reconstructed chariots and furniture. The museum rewards depth over coverage, so your Egyptologist shapes the route around your interests rather than walking every gallery.

**Evening.** Check in at your hotel. The evening is free.

**The day:** Grand Egyptian Museum · Tutankhamun galleries
**Overnight:** Barceló Cairo Pyramids or Pyramids Park Resort
**Meals:** Lunch
**Travel:** Meet and greet at Cairo airport · private air-conditioned transfers
**Pace:** Straight from the airport, then an easy evening

### Day 2 · Giza → Old Cairo
**The pyramids, Coptic Cairo, the Citadel and Khan El Khalili**

**Morning.** The Giza plateau. The pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built within about three generations, around 2500 BC. You go inside the pyramid of Khafre or Menkaure, subject to site rules and availability on the day; a narrow descending passage leads to the burial chamber. Then the panorama viewpoint, where the three pyramids line up in one frame, and the Great Sphinx below, carved from a single outcrop of limestone, with the Valley Temple beside it. The scale is what photographs fail to convey.

**Midday.** Lunch at a local restaurant.

**Afternoon.** Across the city to Old Cairo’s Coptic quarter: the Hanging Church, the church of Saints Sergius and Bacchus (Abu Serga), and the Ben Ezra Synagogue. Then the Citadel of Saladin, built in the 12th century on a spur of the Mokattam hills, with the Mosque of Muhammad Ali, completed in the 1840s, its twin minarets visible from most of the city, and views over Cairo from the walls. The day ends in Khan El Khalili, trading since the 14th century: not a monument to the past, but a city still doing what it has always done.

**The day:** Giza pyramids · inside Khafre or Menkaure · panorama viewpoint · Great Sphinx · Valley Temple · Hanging Church · Abu Serga · Ben Ezra Synagogue · Citadel of Saladin · Mosque of Muhammad Ali · Khan El Khalili
**Overnight:** Barceló Cairo Pyramids or Pyramids Park Resort

### Day 3 · Memphis, Saqqara and Dahshur
**The pyramid fields south of Cairo**

**Morning.** Memphis, with the fallen colossus of Ramses II and a large alabaster sphinx. Then Saqqara, where the pyramid was invented: the Step Pyramid of Djoser, built around 2650 BC by Imhotep, in a necropolis still giving up new finds.

**Midday.** Lunch at a local restaurant.

**Afternoon.** Dahshur, where Sneferu built the Bent Pyramid and the Red Pyramid: the step between Djoser’s steps and the smooth sides of Giza, in a quieter corner of the desert. You go inside the Red Pyramid. Then back to your hotel.

**The day:** Memphis · Colossus of Ramses II · Saqqara · Step Pyramid of Djoser · Dahshur · Bent Pyramid · inside the Red Pyramid
**Overnight:** Barceló Cairo Pyramids or Pyramids Park Resort
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
