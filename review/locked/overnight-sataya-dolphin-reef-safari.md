## Opening

This trip stays out overnight at the reef. A shared boat leaves Hamata, south of Marsa Alam, for Shaab Sataya, a crescent-shaped reef about 45 kilometres offshore whose lagoon is home to a resident pod of spinner dolphins. You snorkel on arrival, again in the afternoon, sleep aboard, and go back in the water at dawn before the sail home.

---

## Why this route works

The dolphins use Sataya’s lagoon to rest and socialise through the day, so time on the reef matters more than anything else, and a night aboard doubles it. The crescent of the reef breaks the swell, which keeps the water inside calm and clear. Staying out also gives you the reef in the evening and at first light, when the day boats have gone.

## Your two days

### Day 1 · Marsa Alam → Hamata → Shaab Sataya
**The road south, the sail out, and the first swim with the dolphins**

A private car collects you from your Marsa Alam hotel for the drive south to Hamata, about two hours depending on where your hotel sits along the coast. The boat leaves the jetty at 08:30, and the sail to Shaab Sataya takes about two hours. Your guide briefs you on how to behave in the water, then leads the first snorkel where the dolphins are active: you swim near them, you never chase them. Lunch aboard, a second session in the afternoon among table corals and sea fans, and dinner on deck at sunset.

**The day:** the drive to Hamata · the sail to Shaab Sataya · two guided snorkelling sessions
**Overnight:** aboard at Shaab Sataya, in a double cabin or on the open deck
**Meals:** Lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private car, Marsa Alam → Hamata · shared boat
**Pace:** An early start, then the day in and out of the water

### Day 2 · Shaab Sataya → Hamata → Marsa Alam
**A dawn swim, then the sail home**

You wake with the reef around you. At first light, with the lagoon quiet, a last guided snorkel, and a second chance with the dolphins if they are close. Breakfast aboard, then the sail back to Hamata, about two hours, and the private car north to your hotel in Marsa Alam, about two hours more. Spinner dolphins are wild animals: on most days they are in the lagoon, but no crew can promise an encounter.

## Is this journey for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- have done the day-return reefs and want more time in the water
- want to see wild dolphins in open sea, not in a pool or a pen
- are happy to share a boat and sleep simply

**Worth knowing:**
- Everyone who goes in the water should be a confident swimmer. This is open sea, far from shore.
- There is no fixed minimum age, but children come only under the close supervision of a parent or guardian.
- Currents can run at Sataya, depending on the tide and wind; your guide chooses the entry points.
- The open deck is pleasant from May to October. If anyone in your party wants a cabin with a door, ask us about cabins when you book.
- Hamata is about two hours’ drive from Marsa Alam, each way, and the boat leaves at 08:30, so Day 1 starts early.
