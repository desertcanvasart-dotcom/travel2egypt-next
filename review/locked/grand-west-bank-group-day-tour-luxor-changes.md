## Product facts
- **Six sites, not five.** Deir el-Medina added, because Sawa's tour ends there (Sawa: 14:50–15:40 slot, "the village and tombs of the royal tomb artisans").
- **Pickup 07:00, back around 16:15, about 9 hours** (Sawa). The old page said 09:00 pickup and about 8 hours.
- **Group size 4 to 8** (Sawa). The `groupSize` field still says "4–12 guests" in all three languages.
- **Included now matches Sawa:** Egyptologist, AC transport, hotel pickup and drop-off, lunch at a local restaurant, entrance tickets. Lunch is new (the old page had none). No water and no driver's tip, because Sawa lists neither.
- **Not included matches Sawa:** special tombs; tipping (Sawa: gratuities).
- **Tutankhamun and Nefertari:** treated as special tombs, not included, as on the private page. Nefertari: closed at present (owner, 22 Nov). Sawa's own itinerary says the opposite; see open items.
- Seti I (KV17) and Ramses VI dropped: Sawa doesn't mention them, and naming tombs with unstated prices invites a promise.
- "Nefertari on request, extra fee and permit" removed everywhere (summary, shape-of-day card, concierge note, options).
- Customisation options (add tombs, pair with East Bank, balloon, private pace) removed: group tours are fixed products. "Make it your own" becomes "Book this trip".
