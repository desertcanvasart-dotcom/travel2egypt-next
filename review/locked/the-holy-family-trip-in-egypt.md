## Opening

According to the Gospel of Matthew, Joseph took Mary and the child to Egypt to escape Herod. Coptic tradition has mapped where they went, place by place: in through Sinai, across the Delta, to a crypt in Old Cairo, and south up the Nile to Deir el-Muharraq. This private journey follows that route, then joins the older Egypt of Luxor, the river and Abu Simbel.

---

## Why this route works

The trail and the temples share one valley, so the route can follow the family's journey in its own order and then carry on south. The Delta and Wadi Natrun days run from one Cairo hotel, so you unpack once for a week. The road south passes the Middle Egypt sites in sequence and reaches Luxor by way of Dendera. From there a four-night cruise takes over, and a flight from Abu Simbel brings you back to Cairo.

### Day 2 · Giza, Memphis and Saqqara
**The Egypt the family would have known as already ancient**

Giza first: the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC, already more than two thousand years old when the family came. You go inside the pyramid of Khafre or Menkaure; then the panorama viewpoint, the Great Sphinx and the Valley Temple. South to Memphis, first capital of a united Egypt, with the fallen colossus of Ramses II, and Saqqara, where the Step Pyramid of Djoser, built around 2650 BC, began the pyramid tradition, with the Imhotep Museum and one of the nobles' tombs.

**The day:** Giza pyramids · inside Khafre or Menkaure · panorama viewpoint · Great Sphinx · Valley Temple · Memphis · Saqqara · Step Pyramid of Djoser · Imhotep Museum · a nobles' tomb
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch

### Day 3 · The Eastern Delta
**Farma, Tell Basta and Belbeis**

An early start east to Farma, ancient Pelusium, the Roman frontier city at Egypt's eastern edge, now ruins near the North Sinai coast, where Coptic tradition says the family entered Egypt. Then back west to Tell Basta, ancient Bubastis, city of the cat goddess Bastet, where tradition places a spring that rose for the family. Last, Belbeis, where they are said to have rested under a tree, before the drive back to Cairo.

**The day:** Farma (Pelusium) · Tell Basta (Bubastis) · Belbeis
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel

### Day 4 · The Central Delta
**Samanoud and Sakha**

North into the farmland of the Delta. Samanoud, on the Damietta branch of the Nile, with the Church of Saint Abanoub, named for a young martyr of the Roman persecutions, where tradition says the family stayed. Then Sakha, where the Church of the Virgin keeps a stone said to bear the footprint of the child Jesus. These are working parish churches rather than monuments, and the communities who keep them are part of the visit. Back to Cairo in the evening.

**The day:** Samanoud · Church of Saint Abanoub · Sakha · Church of the Virgin
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel

### Day 5 · Wadi Natrun
**The desert monasteries**

West from Cairo into the desert to Wadi Natrun, a shallow valley where monks have lived since the 4th century, and where tradition says the family passed. Four monasteries still stand here as working communities. You visit two: Deir Anba Bishoi and Deir el-Suriani, the Monastery of the Syrians, with its old wall paintings. Both are walled, with a keep reached by a drawbridge, built for centuries of raids. Then back to Cairo.

**The day:** Wadi Natrun · Deir Anba Bishoi · Deir el-Suriani
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel

### Day 6 · Coptic Cairo, Matariya and Mostorod
**The crypt at Abu Serga, the Hanging Church and the Virgin's Tree**

Old Cairo, inside the walls of the Roman fortress of Babylon. Abu Serga, the Church of Saints Sergius and Bacchus, keeps the crypt where Coptic tradition says the Holy Family sheltered. The Hanging Church, al-Muallaqa, sits over the fortress gatehouse, its nave dense with icons and carved screens. Then north across the city to Matariya and the Virgin Mary's Tree, a sycamore grown from the line of the tree the family is said to have rested under, and Mostorod, where the Church of the Virgin marks another stop.

**The day:** Abu Serga · Hanging Church · Virgin Mary's Tree, Matariya · Church of the Virgin, Mostorod

### Day 8 · Minya → Deir el-Muharraq → Sohag
**The southern end of the trail**

South to Deir el-Muharraq, in Coptic tradition the southernmost point of the family's journey, where they are said to have lived for six months and ten days before the angel told Joseph to return. The monastery is a working community, and its Church of the Virgin is at the centre of the tradition. Time here is unhurried. Then on south to Sohag for the night.

### Day 9 · Sohag → Dendera → Luxor
**The White Monastery and the Temple of Hathor**

The White Monastery outside Sohag, a 5th-century foundation, partly built from the blocks of older pharaonic monuments. It isn't on the family's route, but it shows what Egyptian Christianity built in the generations after. Then south to Dendera's Temple of Hathor, built mainly in the 1st centuries BC and AD, with its hypostyle hall, astronomical ceiling and roof chapels; a replica of the zodiac stands in place of the original, now in the Louvre. On to Luxor.

**Overnight:** Luxor hotel

### Day 11 · Luxor · the West Bank
**The Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut and the Colossi**

The Valley of the Kings, more than sixty royal tombs cut into the cliffs, their corridors painted with scenes from the Book of the Dead. **The High Dam, Philae and a felucca**

The Aswan High Dam, completed in 1970, and the Unfinished Obelisk, cracked in its quarry. By motorboat to Philae, the Temple of Isis, moved to Agilkia Island in the 1970s. A late-afternoon felucca around Kitchener's Island, planted as a garden in the 1890s.

### Day 14 · Aswan → Abu Simbel → Cairo
**The temples of Ramses II, then the flight north**

Off the cruise after breakfast and a short flight south to Abu Simbel: Ramses II's two cliff temples of the 13th century BC, their four seated figures twenty metres high and the sanctuary reached by the sun twice a year. Between 1964 and 1968 they were cut into blocks and rebuilt 65 metres higher, out of reach of Lake Nasser. Then the flight to Cairo.
