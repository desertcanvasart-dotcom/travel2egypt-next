## Opening

This isn’t a cultural tour with a round of golf added, or a beach holiday with a pyramid excursion for cover. It is eight days for people who play golf and also want to see Egypt properly: one full day for Giza, the Grand Egyptian Museum and the Citadel, a round at Katameya Heights in the hills east of Cairo, and three nights at Jolie Ville Golf & Resort in Sharm El Sheikh for two rounds on a desert course and afternoons on the Red Sea.

---

## Why this route works

It stays in the north and runs efficiently. Cairo’s monuments come first, while you’re fresh, then a round close to the city before the flight to Sinai. The middle of the trip is golf in the mornings and the sea in the afternoons, the heat dictating the order. What you don’t get is Luxor, Aswan or the south; that is a separate journey, and this one doesn’t pretend otherwise.

### Day 2 · Giza and Cairo
**The pyramids, the Grand Egyptian Museum and the Citadel**

Giza first: the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC, with a walk inside Khafre’s or Menkaure’s, the panorama, the Sphinx and the Valley Temple below it. After lunch, the Grand Egyptian Museum on the edge of the plateau, with Tutankhamun’s complete burial collection shown together. The afternoon ends at the Citadel of Saladin and the alabaster Mosque of Muhammad Ali, completed in 1848. A heavy day, on foot over uneven ground.

**The day:** Giza pyramids · inside Khafre’s or Menkaure’s pyramid · Great Sphinx · Valley Temple · Grand Egyptian Museum · Citadel of Saladin · Mosque of Muhammad Ali
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers
**Pace:** A full day on foot

**Optional on this day:**
- Inside the Great Pyramid of Khufu: extra ticket
- A camel ride at Giza: extra

### Day 3 · Katameya Heights
**The New Cairo round**

The third day trades stone for fairway. Katameya Heights sits in the New Cairo hills east of the capital, a course laid into the desert plateau with views across it. The club books your tee time and briefs you on the course; club hire is available, though most serious players bring their own. Lunch at the club, and the afternoon is free.

**The day:** A round at Katameya Heights
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers
**Pace:** A morning on the course, a free afternoon

---

### Day 4 · Cairo → Sharm El Sheikh
**East to Sinai, and the first afternoon on the reef**

A morning flight east over the Gulf of Suez to Sharm El Sheikh, at the southern tip of the Sinai, where the Gulfs of Aqaba and Suez meet. You check in at Jolie Ville Golf & Resort, formerly the Mövenpick, for three nights. The afternoon is for the Red Sea: snorkelling over the reef, a glass-bottom boat, or simply the beach.

**Overnight:** Jolie Ville Golf & Resort, Sharm El Sheikh
**Meals:** Breakfast (in Cairo)
**Travel:** Private transfer to Cairo airport · domestic flight Cairo → Sharm El Sheikh (not included) · private transfer to the resort
**Pace:** A travelling morning, an easy afternoon

---

### Day 5 · Sharm El Sheikh
**The desert course, then the sea**

An early round, before the heat, on the course in the desert hills above the resort, with the Red Sea in view from several holes. It plays differently from Katameya: more exposed and drier, the irrigated fairways sharp against the desert around them. The afternoon is yours on the coast: a semi-submarine with viewing windows below the waterline, snorkelling straight off the reef, or the beach.

**The day:** A round at Sharm El Sheikh
**Overnight:** Jolie Ville Golf & Resort, Sharm El Sheikh
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Resort transfers to the course
**Pace:** A morning round, a free afternoon

**Optional on this day:**
- A semi-submarine on the reef: extra

---

### Day 6 · Sharm El Sheikh
**A second round, and the desert at sunset**

A second morning on the same course, now that you know it. In the late afternoon, a quad bike into the Sinai desert behind the town, for the terrain and the light at that hour, the one part of this trip that belongs neither to the ancient world nor to the sea. Or another afternoon on the reef.

**The day:** A round at Sharm El Sheikh
**Overnight:** Jolie Ville Golf & Resort, Sharm El Sheikh
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Resort transfers to the course
**Pace:** A morning round, a free afternoon

**Optional on this day:**
- A sunset quad-bike trip into the desert: extra

---

### Day 7 · Sharm El Sheikh → Cairo
**Khan el-Khalili, and the Nile after dark**

The flight back to Cairo lands in time for lunch, and the afternoon goes to Khan el-Khalili, the bazaar quarter trading since the late 14th century: spices, copper, textiles and lamps, a working market that rewards wandering rather than a march through. The day ends on the water, with a dinner cruise on the Nile past the lit bridges and the Corniche.

**The day:** Khan el-Khalili · dinner cruise on the Nile
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast (at the resort) · lunch · dinner (on the dinner cruise)
**Travel:** Domestic flight Sharm El Sheikh → Cairo (not included) · private transfers
