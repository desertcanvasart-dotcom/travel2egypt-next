## Opening

This is a sail, not a sightseeing tour. No temples, no tickets, no commentary track: about three hours on a felucca at Aswan, where the Nile runs narrower and darker between granite outcrops and the long green line of Elephantine Island, ending with lunch at a table set on a sandy beach by the water. The boat is yours alone, the wind sets the pace, and your Nubian captain may well sing.

---

## Why the day runs this way

Aswan’s monuments (Philae, the High Dam, the Unfinished Obelisk) fill their own day. This one belongs to the river. We recommend it from November to March, when the weather is at its best and the northerly breeze is at its most reliable. If the wind drops on the day, a motorboat tows the felucca, so the sail still goes ahead. Tell us how your Aswan days look, and we’ll set the departure time to suit.

## Your day

### On the Nile

**The boat dock on the Corniche**
You meet your captain and assistant at the boat dock in front of the EgyptAir office on the Corniche, an easy landmark to find in Aswan.

**Under sail**
A traditional wooden felucca with a single triangular sail. You cast off and the city falls behind: both banks, the palm-fringed islands mid-channel, and the granite shoreline where the first cataract begins, just south of the city. You won’t reach the cataract itself. Fishermen work the water in small boats, and herons stand on the rocks. Your assistant talks about the felucca and the river as much or as little as you like, and the captain may sing the Nubian songs of the river.
*Sites:* Aswan Corniche · Elephantine Island (from the water)

### Lunch on a Nile beach

**Lunch at a table on a sandy beach (included)**
The sail ends at a sandy beach on the river, where lunch is served at a proper table and chairs: freshly baked Egyptian bread with baba ghanoush and tahini, then grilled chicken or meat with rice and seasonal vegetables. Drinks aren’t included.

**Back to the dock**
After lunch, back to the boat dock on the Corniche, about three hours after you set off.

## Is this day for you?

**A good fit if you:**
- have seen, or will see, Aswan’s monuments and want one unstructured half-day on the river
- would rather watch the water than follow a commentary
- are travelling as a family

**Worth knowing:**
- We recommend it from November to March, for the weather and the wind.
- If there’s no wind, a motorboat tows the felucca.
- The felucca has no shade canopy. The sun you sail under is the sun you get, so bring a hat and sunscreen.
- It moves with the wind, never fast. Restless children, or anyone who wants structured commentary throughout, may find three hours long.
- Your assistant speaks English.
- Getting there: the tour starts at the boat dock in front of the EgyptAir office on the Corniche. A transfer from your hotel can be added at extra cost.
