## Opening

Ten days that start in the south and go with the current: two in Aswan, two nights on a Nile yacht past Kom Ombo and Edfu, then Luxor with time to spare. No Cairo, and several days left open for you to fill or leave alone.

---

## Why this route works

Many trips begin in Cairo. This one begins in Aswan, smaller and slower, and lets Egypt grow around you. The yacht moves north to Luxor, where five nights in one hotel leave room for the great sites and for the days after them.

## Your ten days

### Day 1 · Aswan
**Arrival**

You’re met at Aswan airport and taken to your five-star hotel on the Nile. The rest of the day is easy.

**Overnight:** Aswan hotel
**Travel:** Meet and greet at the airport · private transfer
**Pace:** Easy

---

### Day 2 · Aswan
**The High Dam, the Unfinished Obelisk and Philae**

The High Dam, completed in 1970. The Unfinished Obelisk, left in its quarry when the granite cracked. Philae, the island temple of Isis, taken apart and rebuilt on higher ground to save it from the water, and reached by boat.

**The day:** Aswan High Dam · Unfinished Obelisk · Temple of Philae
**Overnight:** Aswan hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers · motorboat to Philae

**Optional on this day:**
- Abu Simbel by air, with your guide: extra
- The Sound and Light Show at Philae: extra

---

### Day 3 · Aswan
**Aboard the yacht**

After breakfast you board a Nile yacht, such as the Alexander the Great, and settle into your cabin. There is time for Aswan before the yacht turns north.

**Overnight:** Nile yacht
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner

**Optional on this day:**
- A felucca around Kitchener’s Island: extra
- The Nubian Museum: extra
- A Nubian village: extra

---

### Day 4 · Kom Ombo → Edfu
**The river temples**

Kom Ombo, on a bend of the river, built in two matching halves for Sobek and Horus the Elder. Then the temple of Horus at Edfu, built between 237 and 57 BC, with its walls, roof and sanctuary still standing.

**The day:** Kom Ombo · Temple of Horus at Edfu
**Overnight:** Nile yacht
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner

---

### Day 5 · Luxor
**Arrive in ancient Thebes**

The yacht reaches Luxor and you move to your five-star hotel. The day is yours.

**Overnight:** Luxor hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Private transfer
**Pace:** Easy

**Optional on this day:**
- Luxor Temple after dark: extra

---

### Day 6 · Luxor
**The East Bank**

Karnak, enlarged by pharaoh after pharaoh over some fifteen centuries, with its Great Hypostyle Hall of 134 columns. Luxor Temple, joined to it by the Avenue of Sphinxes, and the Luxor Museum.

**The day:** Karnak · Luxor Temple · Luxor Museum
**Overnight:** Luxor hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers

**Optional on this day:**
- The Sound and Light Show at Karnak: extra

---

### Day 7 · Luxor
**The West Bank**

The Valley of the Kings, with more than sixty royal tombs, Tutankhamun’s among them. The terraced temple of Hatshepsut at Deir el-Bahari, and the Colossi of Memnon.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon
**Overnight:** Luxor hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers

**Optional on this day:**
- The tombs of Tutankhamun, Seti I and Ramesses III: extra tickets

---

### Day 8 · Luxor

Return somewhere, go somewhere new, or stay by the river.

**Overnight:** Luxor hotel

**Overnight:** Luxor hotel

**Optional on this day:**

Breakfast, and your transfer to Luxor airport.

**Overnight:** —
