## Opening

The Egypt trip most families want, done the way that works: three days in Cairo, a flight south, four nights on a five-star Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan, and a flight home. The pyramids, the Grand Egyptian Museum, Karnak’s neighbour Luxor Temple, the Valley of the Kings and Philae, with a rhythm children actually like.

---

## Why this route works

Long enough to do Cairo properly, and a cruise that builds rather than rushes: a temple in the morning, pool and lunch, sailing in the afternoon. The ship is shared, but the shore time and the guiding are yours alone, with an Egyptologist briefed for your children’s ages.

### Day 2 · Cairo
**Giza, the Grand Egyptian Museum and a Nile dinner cruise**

**Morning.** The pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC, and the Sphinx, with the panorama from the higher viewpoint.

**Afternoon.** The Grand Egyptian Museum, with all 5,000-plus pieces of Tutankhamun’s collection on one route; your guide paces it for the children and lets the parents linger where they want.

**Evening.** A Nile dinner cruise with live music and the first proper view of the skyline from the water.

**The day:** Giza pyramids · Great Sphinx · Grand Egyptian Museum · Nile dinner cruise
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner (on the dinner cruise)
**Travel:** Private transfers

**Optional on this day:**
- A short camel ride or a horse-drawn carriage along the back of the plateau. We work with handlers we know, whose animals are in good condition, and the ride is entirely your choice.

### Day 3 · Cairo
**The Citadel, Old Cairo and Khan El Khalili**

The Citadel of Saladin and the Mosque of Muhammad Ali, with its domes, courtyards and long view over the city. The Coptic quarter: the Hanging Church and the Ben Ezra Synagogue. In the evening, the lanes of Khan El Khalili and Al-Muizz Street.

**The day:** Citadel of Saladin · Mosque of Muhammad Ali · Hanging Church · Ben Ezra Synagogue · Khan El Khalili · Al-Muizz Street
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Private transfers

**Optional on this day:**
- The Children’s Museum, interactive and hands-on, for a calmer afternoon
- The royal mummies at the National Museum of Egyptian Civilisation

> More about this stop → *(expands the essay’s Cairo passage)*

### Day 4 · Cairo → Luxor
**Board the cruise, and Luxor Temple at dusk**

A short flight south to Luxor, boarding at midday and lunch on board. The first visit is Luxor Temple at dusk, its floodlit columns in the middle of the modern city, dramatic enough to hold the children’s attention after a travel day.

**The day:** Luxor Temple at dusk
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Luxor
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Domestic flight Cairo → Luxor · private transfers

---

### Day 5 · Luxor · the West Bank
**The Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut and a hieroglyphics workshop**

The Valley of the Kings, with three tombs chosen for the children’s ages: the painted ceiling of Seti I, the corridor of Ramses VI, and a third to suit their interests. Hatshepsut’s terraced temple at Deir el-Bahari, which children take to once they know her story, and a quick stop at the Colossi of Memnon. Pool time on board in the afternoon, and in the evening, a hieroglyphics workshop where the children write their names in the pharaohs’ script and take the cartouche home.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon · hieroglyphics workshop
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfers

**Optional on this day:**
- The tomb of Tutankhamun: extra ticket

### Day 6 · Edfu and Kom Ombo → Aswan
**The falcon god and the crocodile god**

The ship sails south overnight. In the morning, Edfu’s Temple of Horus, the falcon god, buried for centuries and dug out in the 19th century, with sharp reliefs and a treasure-card game for younger children. In the afternoon, Kom Ombo’s twin temple for Sobek the crocodile and Horus, and the crocodile museum children remember. Aswan by evening, with an optional galabiya dress-up night.

**The day:** Temple of Horus at Edfu · Temple of Kom Ombo · Crocodile Museum
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Aswan
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** By cruise

### Day 7 · Aswan
**The High Dam, the Unfinished Obelisk, Philae and a Nubian village**

The High Dam, completed in 1970, and what it cost and gave. The Unfinished Obelisk, left where the granite cracked, the nearest the children will get to watching ancient masons at work. Philae, the temple of Isis, by motor launch. Then a felucca around Elephantine Island and a visit to a brightly painted Nubian village, where children meet children. A farewell dinner on board, with Nubian music and dance.

**The day:** Aswan High Dam · Unfinished Obelisk · Temple of Philae · felucca · Nubian village · Nubian music and dance
**Overnight:** Nile cruise ship, Aswan
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Private transfers · motor launch · felucca

**Optional on this day:**
- Abu Simbel by air from Aswan

## Your stay and travel

**Hotels:** three nights at a family-friendly Cairo hotel such as the Pyramids Park Resort, bed and breakfast.

**Flights:** Cairo to Luxor, and Aswan to Cairo, with airport assistance.

**Every day:** your own licensed Egyptologist in your language, briefed for your children’s ages, and private transfers.
