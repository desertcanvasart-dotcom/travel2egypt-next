# Changes from the old page: Nubian Life

- **Format:** old essay page → the day-tour format, modelled on the approved Aswan felucca page (boat product, assistant rather than Egyptologist, "Starting point" and "With you" rows).
- **Title and URL:** unchanged.
- **Tiers removed** (the old concierge line mentioned tiers; the price is one per-person price by group size).
- **Minimum age 10 removed**; "Children under 12: 50% off" added. The old page said both "minimum age is ten" and "travels comfortably with children".
- **Guide:** kept as an English-speaking assistant, as on the old page (open item).
- **New inclusions:** tea in a Nubian home (the old route had it; the old inclusions didn't list it) and a bottle of water (old shape-of-day: "mineral water provided"). Both open items.
- **New exclusions:** hotel transfers, purchases, tipping.
- **History corrected:** the old page said the villages around Aswan, "Gharb Soheil principally", were built by people resettled after the High Dam. Gharb Soheil lies north of the dams and wasn't flooded; most resettled Egyptian Nubians went to the Kom Ombo area. The draft now says the old homeland further south was flooded and resettled, and that villages like this keep its language, building and colour.
- **"Nubian is a distinct language family"** → "a language of its own, not a dialect of Arabic".
- **Boat time** "fifteen to twenty minutes" left out (open item).
- **"see the school"** left out (not something we can promise on every visit).
- **Closing line** "The houses are painted because the homeland was lost" → new line: the painting tradition is older than the displacement.
- **Dropped:** "one of the largest forced relocations in twentieth-century Egypt", "one of the few Aswan trips that travels comfortably with children" (comparison).
