## Opening

Cairo first: Saqqara, Old Cairo, the Egyptian Museum and the pyramids at Giza. Then a flight south to Aswan and Abu Simbel, three nights on a dahabiya down the river to Luxor, and three nights by the Red Sea at Hurghada before the flight back to Cairo. You travel with a small group of four to eight people and one licensed Egyptologist.

---

## Why this route works

The dahabiya, a traditional Nile sailing boat with a few cabins, goes with the current from Aswan to Luxor, so the river does the travelling. From Luxor the Red Sea is a drive, not a flight, and after the temples the three days at the coast are free time. The trip ends with an evening in old Cairo, near the airport for your flight home.

## Your twelve days

### Day 1 · Cairo
**Arrival**

You're met at Cairo airport, helped through arrival formalities and taken to your hotel. In the evening, a welcome dinner with your group at a local restaurant.

**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Dinner
**Travel:** Meet and assist at the airport, transfer to the hotel

---

### Day 2 · Memphis, Saqqara and Old Cairo
**Where the pyramid began, and the city's Christian quarter**

Memphis, first capital of a united Egypt around 3100 BC, with the fallen colossus of Ramses II. Saqqara and the Step Pyramid of Djoser, built around 2650 BC, the first pyramid and the experiment that made Giza possible. After lunch at a local restaurant, Old Cairo: the Hanging Church, built over a gate of the Roman fortress of Babylon, and the lanes of the Coptic quarter.

**The day:** Memphis · Saqqara · Step Pyramid of Djoser · Hanging Church · Coptic Cairo
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch
**Travel:** Transfers with your group

---

### Day 3 · The Egyptian Museum and Giza
**The pharaohs' collection, then the pyramids**

The Egyptian Museum on Tahrir Square, opened in 1902, with its collection from every period of ancient Egypt. Then the Giza plateau: the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built around 2500 BC, the Great Sphinx and the Valley Temple below it. Dinner with your group at a local restaurant.

**The day:** Egyptian Museum · Giza pyramids · Great Sphinx · Valley Temple
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · dinner
**Travel:** Transfers with your group

**Optional on this day:**
- Inside one of the pyramids: extra ticket

---

### Day 4 · Cairo → Aswan → Abu Simbel
**The temples of Ramses II, then a felucca at sunset**

An early flight to Aswan, and on by air to Abu Simbel: Ramses II's two cliff temples of the 13th century BC, their four seated figures twenty metres high. Between 1964 and 1968 they were cut into blocks and rebuilt 65 metres higher, out of reach of Lake Nasser. Back in Aswan by air, a felucca on the river as the sun goes down behind the west bank.

**The day:** Abu Simbel · Great Temple of Ramses II · Temple of Nefertari · felucca
**Overnight:** Aswan hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Domestic flights Cairo → Aswan and Aswan ⇄ Abu Simbel (not included) · transfers · felucca
**Pace:** A long day

---

### Day 5 · Aswan
**The High Dam, the Unfinished Obelisk, Philae, and on board**

The Aswan High Dam, completed in 1970, which made Lake Nasser. The Unfinished Obelisk, still lying in its granite quarry where it cracked. By motorboat to Philae, the Temple of Isis, moved to Agilkia Island above the new waterline. Then on board your dahabiya, moored in Aswan for the night.

**The day:** Aswan High Dam · Unfinished Obelisk · Temple of Philae
**Overnight:** Dahabiya, Aswan
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Transfers · motorboat

---

### Day 6 · Kom Ombo
**A double temple on a bend of the river**

The dahabiya sails north. Kom Ombo, its plan doubled for Sobek and Horus the Elder, with mummified crocodiles in the museum. Then on toward Edfu.

**The day:** Temple of Kom Ombo · Crocodile Museum
**Overnight:** Dahabiya
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** By dahabiya

---

### Day 7 · Edfu → Luxor · the East Bank
**The Temple of Horus, the Esna lock, and Karnak**

Edfu's Temple of Horus, its enclosure wall, roof, halls and sanctuary standing as completed in the 1st century BC. Back on board, through the lock at Esna to Luxor. In the afternoon, Karnak, a temple city added to over fifteen centuries, with the Great Hypostyle Hall of 134 columns up to 21 metres, and Luxor Temple by the river.

**The day:** Temple of Horus at Edfu · Esna lock · Karnak · Great Hypostyle Hall · Luxor Temple
**Overnight:** Dahabiya, Luxor
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** By dahabiya · transfers

---

### Day 8 · Luxor → Hurghada
**The West Bank, then east to the Red Sea**

Off the dahabiya after breakfast. The Valley of the Kings, more than sixty royal tombs cut into the cliffs. Hatshepsut's colonnaded temple at Deir el-Bahari, and the Colossi of Memnon, two seated figures of Amenhotep III about 18 metres tall. Then about four hours across the Eastern Desert to Hurghada, where your all-inclusive hotel starts with lunch.

**The day:** Valley of the Kings · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon
**Overnight:** Hurghada hotel, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Travel:** Transfers · road Luxor → Hurghada
**Pace:** A long day

**Optional on this day:**
- A hot-air balloon over the West Bank at sunrise: extra
- The tombs of Tutankhamun, Seti I and Ramses VI: extra tickets

---

### Day 9 · Hurghada
**A free day by the Red Sea**

The day is yours: the beach, the pool, or a boat to the islands and the reefs.

**Overnight:** Hurghada hotel, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** Free day

**Optional on this day:**
- Snorkelling, diving or a boat trip to the islands: extra

---

### Day 10 · Hurghada
**Another free day on the coast**

Snorkel over the coral, walk the marina in the evening, or stay by the pool.

**Overnight:** Hurghada hotel, all-inclusive
**Meals:** Breakfast · lunch · dinner
**Pace:** Free day

---

### Day 11 · Hurghada → Cairo
**Khan El Khalili, and a farewell dinner**

The flight back to Cairo and to your hotel. In the evening, Khan El Khalili, the bazaar founded in the 14th century, its lanes of copper, spice and coffee houses, then a farewell dinner with your group at a local restaurant.

**The day:** Khan El Khalili
**Overnight:** Cairo hotel
**Meals:** Breakfast · dinner
**Travel:** Domestic flight Hurghada → Cairo (not included) · transfers

---

### Day 12 · Cairo
**Departure**

Breakfast and the transfer to Cairo airport.

**Overnight:** —
**Meals:** Breakfast
**Travel:** Transfer to the airport
