# Old-format group packages: audit, 27 November 2026

Read from production Sanity on 27 Nov. Prices are not reviewed here; the owner sets them later.

Three group packages are still in the old format: the 9-day, the 10-day and the 12-day.

## Problems common to all three

1. **Visitors see only the long essay.** The day-by-day rows, inclusions and meals were extracted automatically and never approved (`needsReview: true`). The site hides unapproved rows, so the itinerary, inclusions and exclusions never show as structured blocks.
2. **Flights.** All three use domestic flights. Owner rule: flights are never in the price. The 12-day page says domestic flights are included, and the 9-day and 10-day pages are silent. Sawa's packages do include domestic flights.
3. **No "Not included" list** on any of the three.
4. **Past departure dates** are still stored: the 9-day has 31 Oct and 14 Nov; the 10-day has 9 Nov and 25 Nov. Dates are moving to Sawa anyway.
5. **Unverifiable comparative claims:**
   - 9-day: "a night at Abu Simbel almost no one else offers", "almost no group tour offers".
   - 12-day: "the Dahabiya that most group tours never offer", "what separates this departure from every conventional cruise itinerary".
6. **No cities set** on the 9-day and 10-day, so neither page links to its city guides or hubs.

## 9-Day: Cairo, Abu Simbel & the Nile (`/9-day-egypt-group-tour-cairo-abu-simbel-nile`)

The route runs Cairo → sleeper train → Abu Simbel overnight → Aswan → 3-night cruise → Luxor hotel → fly to Cairo → Memphis, Saqqara and Dahshur → departure.

- **Karnak and Luxor Temple are missing entirely.** A 9-day Nile trip that skips both is the biggest content gap here.
- **The cruise days don't match how ships run.** The page puts Kom Ombo on Day 5 and Edfu on Day 6. Aswan–Luxor cruises usually visit Kom Ombo on the first evening, Edfu the next morning, then sail through the Esna lock to Luxor.
- **Day 4 is very heavy:** sunrise at Abu Simbel, a 3.5-hour drive, Philae, a felucca, then boarding the cruise.
- **Inclusions:** only 3 extracted. The page never says whether the sleeper train, the Abu Simbel hotel or the Luxor–Cairo flight are included.

## 10-Day: Cairo, the Nile & the Red Sea (`/10-day-egypt-group-tour-cairo-nile-red-sea`)

- **Day 7 is overloaded:** disembark, both banks of Luxor, then a 3.5–4-hour drive to Hurghada. The page admits it is "the fullest day".
- **Same cruise-order problem** as the 9-day: Kom Ombo on Day 5, Edfu on Day 6.
- **Text written for families** on a group tour: "the part of Egypt the children … talk about longest".
- **Vague meals:** "Day 1 dinner, depending on arrival time" and "per the hotel's board basis".
- **Flights:** Cairo–Aswan and Hurghada–Cairo are not stated as included or not.

## 12-Day: Cairo, the Nile by Dahabiya & the Red Sea (`/12-day-egypt-group-tour-cairo-dahabiya-red-sea`)

- **The dahabiya timing is probably not workable.**
  - Day 5: Abu Simbel by road (about 7 hours there and back), then sail to Kom Ombo the same day.
  - Day 6: Edfu in the morning, then Karnak and Luxor Temple the same afternoon.
  - Edfu to Luxor is about 110 km of river including the Esna lock. A dahabiya normally takes 4–5 nights from Aswan to Luxor, not 3.
- **Extras promised at no charge:** "Nothing … held back as an extra" covers the introductory dive, windsurfing, kitesurfing, the submarine and the desert safari. Each needs a costing check.
- **Flights:** "domestic flights" are listed as included, against the owner's rule.
- **Day 3 contradiction:** the meals line says dinner, but the text says "the evening is free".
- **Day 7:** "additional access arranged for Tutankhamun … when open" doesn't say whether it is included.
- The 12-day does have cities set.
